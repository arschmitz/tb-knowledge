# Account Hub header button listeners share the guarded shadow-tree lifetime

The header creates its shadow tree once and installs click listeners on buttons inside that tree. Its existing-shadowRoot guard skips later initialization, and this patch removes the disconnected listener-removal method. The container also guards setup, connects its own localization root before appending content, and separately disconnects/deletes localization on disconnection. That source structure does not prove a supported reconnect lifecycle or a leak. The component ownership and localization paths must be described separately.

Scope: Account Hub component initialization and cleanup.
