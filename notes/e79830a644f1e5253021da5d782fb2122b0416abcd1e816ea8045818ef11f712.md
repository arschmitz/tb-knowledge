# An awaited revocation call can resolve without revoking the key

Accepted RNP.revokeKey returns early if its unlock tracker is unavailable or remains locked. EnigRevokeKey awaits that call and then unconditionally calls callbackFunc(true) and shows the success alert. Thus promise fulfillment alone does not distinguish those early no-operation outcomes from completed revocation in the inspected UI path. Proposed focused check: unavailable-key and canceled/failed-unlock cases must have an explicit intended UI outcome. Native revoke errors instead throw; no cancellation or error case was executed, and this concern is not attributed to removing the unused stub. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: OpenPGP revocation outcome.
