# Folder listener names identify two different notification systems

The folder chapter distinguishes nsIFolderListener from nsIMsgFolderListener. The former attaches to an individual folder or registers globally through nsIMsgMailSession. The latter registers with nsIMsgFolderNotificationService and supplies interest flags. The checked service dispatch tests those flags before calling a listener. Choose the family by its registration and event contract, not by the similar names. This static check does not establish event order, native callback delivery or every producer.

Scope: Folder architecture listener registration and dispatch.
