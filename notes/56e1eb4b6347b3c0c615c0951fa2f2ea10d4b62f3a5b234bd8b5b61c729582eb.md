# Local transfer status describes the operation instead of repeatedly counting files

The landing replaces x-of-y formatting and progress-bar updates with imapMovingMessages/imapCopyingMessages plus destination name. Current DisplayMoveCopyStatusMsg still formats those two strings but now reports through Feedback::Service rather than historical per-window status feedback. Preserve the current service boundary and distinguish this in-progress message from a final activity count. No speed or visual progress claim is validated here. This is static source and test-code inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending; source landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.
