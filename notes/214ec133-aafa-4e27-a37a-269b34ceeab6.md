# Display one notification selected by severity

The first NotificationManager chooses one candidate at a time, sorted by severity. It keeps the current item when it remains eligible and is at least as important as the new first candidate, then emits clear/new events when the chosen item changes. The May 2024 manager also schedules a new choice after an interaction or expiry. Current code has additional scheduling and rate limits.

Scope: in-app notification manager.
