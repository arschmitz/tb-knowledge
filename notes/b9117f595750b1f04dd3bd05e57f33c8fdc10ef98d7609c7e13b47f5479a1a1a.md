# Exit the MAPI sending wait when account shutdown starts

The blind MAPI send path holds refcounted account-manager and send-listener references and spins until the listener is done or account shutdown is in progress. This prevents the wait from requiring a completion callback during teardown. Offline sending still takes a separate early return. Preserve this shutdown escape condition, without treating it as proof that delivery completed or that every shutdown crash is fixed. The commit reports a crash motivation; Windows/MAPI behavior was not reproduced. Complete Bugzilla and historical Phabricator timeline/inline discussion remains pending.

Scope: mailnews / mapi.
