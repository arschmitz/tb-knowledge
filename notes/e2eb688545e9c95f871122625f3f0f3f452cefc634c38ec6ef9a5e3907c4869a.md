# Paired background images can use light-dark while keeping their separate loading background

The watermark variable and Account Hub background-image store the existing light/dark URL pair in light-dark(). Account Central consumes its variable as background-image. Account Hub retains a solid surface color while its WebP loads, plus its sizing and position rules. The applicable document color-scheme remains a dependency; these expressions alone do not prove image loading, painting or contrast. No visual test ran.

Scope: Account Central and Account Hub theme images.
