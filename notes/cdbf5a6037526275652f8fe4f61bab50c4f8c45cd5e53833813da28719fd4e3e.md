# The first compaction failure selects the final alert and listener status

Batch completion takes mFailed[0] and mFailedCodes[0] to choose its alert and final OnStopRunningUrl status. NS_MSG_ERROR_BLOCKED_COMPACTION deliberately has no alert branch, but remains the returned failure. Other failures are logged; the first-failure ordering therefore matters when interpreting a quiet mixed batch. This follows the inspected consumer and does not claim a reproduced lost-alert defect. A useful unexecuted test would vary the order of blocked and real write failures. No compaction ran.
