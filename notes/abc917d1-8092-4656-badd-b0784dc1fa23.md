# Keep tab opening intent separate from its default preference

The May 2024 default changed mail.tabs.loadInBackground to false so ordinary message opening focuses its new tab. The follow-up makes context-menu and middle-click opening explicitly background, with Shift reversing the chosen behavior. OpenMessageInNewTab applies Shift even when the caller sets background; do not let the preference override an explicit action.

Scope: message tab focus.
