# Treat QR snapshots as renderer stability checks with explicit limits

The QR tests compare deterministic output and stored snapshots, including four accounts that generate two codes and a multibyte input. Their comment explicitly says the snapshot does not decode the encoded JSON and assumes an earlier accepted manual check. The fixture inspection here verified counts, URI prefixes, lengths and raw hashes only. A matching snapshot can catch serialization/render changes, but cannot alone prove a phone imports the correct account data. Separate direct getAccountData/getQRData shape assertions from a decoder or cross-device test.

Scope: mail/base/test.
