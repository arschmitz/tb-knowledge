# Recipient pill validity combines a trailing-punctuation check with list and news exemptions

The pill UI check rejects the listed final punctuation .,:;!?-, then updatePillStatus exempts resolved mailing lists and newsgroups before applying invalid-address. Its regex is a limited UI heuristic, not comprehensive email syntax validation. The direct browser fixture tests a trailing dot by committing with Tab and finding the resulting invalid pill; it does not test every punctuation or malformed edge value. No test execution.

Scope: compose-recipient-pill-status.
