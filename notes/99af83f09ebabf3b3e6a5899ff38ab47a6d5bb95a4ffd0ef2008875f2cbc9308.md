# Exclude grouped dummy rows from saved message selection

Grouped-by-sort dummy rows identify a group header; asking their message key/URI can yield a real member that was never selected. SaveSelection filters isGroupedByHeaderAtIndex for both current and selected rows. Restore expands actual saved messages according to its expand option and filters absent indices. Preserve the row-kind distinction when changing selection storage.

Scope: view.
