# Deleting an address book invalidates display-name lookup state

Current deleteAddressBook removes the directory, refreshes the sorted list and increments mail.displayname.version. The regression test checks the version change and absent card after removing the containing book. This extends the invalidation contract beyond editing or deleting individual cards; do not leave a card from a deleted book in the whole-address cache. Existing lessons cover the broader clearCache-before-card-notification contract. This is static source and test-code inspection. Full Bugzilla and review timeline and inline discussion remains pending. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: Address-book deletion invalidation.
