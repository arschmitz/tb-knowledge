# The newsrc parser no longer preserves unused cross-newsreader lines

The paired removals delete unsubscribedNewsgroupLines and optionLines storage. Fixed HandleNewsrcLine still parses subscribed groups and their read sets, but ignores options, unparsed and unsubscribed lines instead of keeping them for a later roundtrip. Review explains that the old attempt to share one newsrc file with other newsreaders is no longer a reason to retain this unused state. That scoped decision does not remove subscribed state or establish every writer contract. No NNTP or cross-reader test ran.

Scope: Newsrc compatibility state.
