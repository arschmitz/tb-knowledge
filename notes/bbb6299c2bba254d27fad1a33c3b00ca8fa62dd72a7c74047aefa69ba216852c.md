# The notification cap test widens request-event tolerance without delaying production

The test keeps its 10-second lower bound and changes the upper bound to 14 seconds. It measures after REQUEST_NOTIFICATIONS_EVENT, whose production timer is based on the oldest shown-history timestamp plus _PER_TIME_UNIT. The change gives the test four seconds of lateness tolerance; it does not add a production wait. The test's constant true assertion and its shown wording do not directly verify a seventh notification display. This is a static event/assertion distinction; no timing or CI result was reproduced.

Scope: In-app notification unit-test timing.
