# Account Hub animated-label clearance follows the complete label height

At the accepted revision, input controls with an animated label reserve top space from --hub-form-label-height minus input block padding. Label height includes its line height and twice its padding. The earlier patch introduced a font-size-only calculation; do not attribute the later height formula to it. Keep this scope limited to Account Hub animated-label containers. Static source does not establish rendered results at enlarged font sizes.

Scope: Account Hub form layout.
