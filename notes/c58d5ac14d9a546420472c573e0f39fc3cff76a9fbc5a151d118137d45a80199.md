# A short summary can keep two conditional outcomes together

The client method uses an existing client or creates one. Its short summary names both outcomes clearly. Keep concise combined wording when it is clear; a suggested condition-first sentence form is not a requirement to expand every summary. This is a scoped source example, not proof that every pronoun or contraction is suitable for generated text.
