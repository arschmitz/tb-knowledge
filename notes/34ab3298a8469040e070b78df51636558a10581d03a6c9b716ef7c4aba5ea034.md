# Open archive options through its real account-settings button

The archive test installs the sub-dialog callback before clicking the account settings button, waits for document readiness or load, then inspects the embedded dialog. This exercises the real gSubDialog opener and identity arguments, rather than constructing a standalone window. Recommend keeping those ownership and readiness boundaries in this test family; an arbitrary openDialog replacement would test a different route. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run.

Scope: mail/test/browser.
