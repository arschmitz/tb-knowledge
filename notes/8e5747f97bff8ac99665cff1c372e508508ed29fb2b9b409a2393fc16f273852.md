# The outgoing-server test prepares localization, focus and scroll before opening its menu

The account-settings test obtains contentFrame.contentDocument, waits for pending localization mutations, focuses that document window, and scrolls the outgoing-server menu into view before clicking it. The fixed source keeps this order with documentGlobal and later menupopup activation. The author reports headless improvement, but source inspection does not isolate scrolling as the cause or establish a fresh test result.
