# The vCard 3 date converters retain separators in serialized values

The vCard 3 date and date-time designs override toICAL with identity functions. Their comments specify retained date hyphens and date-time colons. ICAL.stringify.value dispatches to the design converter, while Thunderbird's VCardProperties.toVCard supplies its jCal entries to ICAL.stringify. The selected current tests are narrower than direct coverage of both converter changes.

Scope: Thunderbird vCard 3 serialization.
