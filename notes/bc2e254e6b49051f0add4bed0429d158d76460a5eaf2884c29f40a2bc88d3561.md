# Shared mail-test cleanup preserves the module-load baseline and includes outgoing servers

CleanupHelpers captures incoming-server, account and outgoing-server keys when the module initializes. removeServersAndAccounts reports and removes only keys outside those saved lists, including outgoing servers. The account head calls it from the retained final diagnostic cleanup. Keep the module-load baseline and test-owned explicit cleanup separate; calling the helper does not reset its baseline. No test-order or leak result was executed here.

Scope: Mail browser-test resource cleanup.
