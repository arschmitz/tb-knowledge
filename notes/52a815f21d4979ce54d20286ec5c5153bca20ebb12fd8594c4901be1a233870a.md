# Keep persisted notification state only for known active or offline IDs

Historical378 pruned seeds/interacted IDs against downloaded notifications. Current cleanup retains the union of downloaded and offline default IDs and supports skipCleanup for replacement states. Removing offline IDs would forget interaction history and may redisplay a notification. Keep storage retention and display filtering separate.

Scope: notifications.
