# Register the image-dialog promise before opening or editing the image

The image test registers promiseAlertDialog before its menu action or image double-click and awaits that promise before inspecting the editor. A scoped test rule is to attach the completion listener before the action that can open the dialog. The small follow-up adds task-turn yields around radio interaction and dialog completion; those yields do not establish a universal timing delay or a confirmed cure for the reported timeout. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Compose image dialog listener order.
