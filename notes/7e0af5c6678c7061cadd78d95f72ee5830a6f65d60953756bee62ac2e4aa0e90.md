# Keep secure fixture filenames descriptive within their test data family

For the checked security tests, fixture paths encode recipient, signature/encryption role and sometimes key ID or digest. The S/MIME family uses dot-separated alice.sig.SHA256.opaque.env.eml; OpenPGP uses descriptive dashed .eml names under data/eml and key material under data/keys. Recommend matching the nearby family when adding fixtures, rather than applying one separator policy across both subsystems. These are observed local names, not a reviewer-approved project-wide rule. These are fixed accepted source and test-code observations. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending.
