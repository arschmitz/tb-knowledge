# DisableExperimentalFeatures hides selected settings without changing their feature flags

The selected policy locks the settings-offer preference false when enabled. General and Calendar pane initialization hide their compiled experimental wrappers; the policy does not turn off the separate conversation or Calendar feature preferences. The browser test directly checks wrapper hidden state with the offer preference false, while xpcshell case data checks expected preference value and locking. Accepted source later renames the offer preference; no runtime feature-disable or accessibility result was established.

Scope: Enterprise policy and experimental settings wrappers.
