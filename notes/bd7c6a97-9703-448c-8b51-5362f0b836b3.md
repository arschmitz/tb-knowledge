# Check recovery paths after renaming state used by normal initialization

The May SmartMailbox refactor renamed the folder pane field from _smartServer to _smartMailbox in init, but regenerateMode still checked _smartServer. With a damaged virtual-folder database, the false guard skipped SmartMailboxUtils.removeAll(true), then init reused the broken state and unified folders could not open. Bug 1927048 fixed the guard to _smartMailbox in January 2025 and was marked as a regression from Bug 1893700. Include corrupt-state recovery and regeneration when reviewing a field rename, not only clean-profile startup.

Scope: smart mailbox recovery.
