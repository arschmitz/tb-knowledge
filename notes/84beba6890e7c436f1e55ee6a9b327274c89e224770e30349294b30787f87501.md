# The deletion test samples scroll direction at scrollend and removes the same listener

The smooth-scroll deletion test registers its direction-check listener for scrollend and removes that same event/listener pair afterward. This samples the final scroll position rather than every scroll event. Its separate promise helper already waits for scrollend and then a deliberate stabilization delay; this patch does not remove the scenario delays or replace the independent select-event waits. Non-strict direction comparisons and visible-row assertions have different limits; they do not establish monotonic movement at every intermediate frame.

Scope: thread-tree-browser-tests.
