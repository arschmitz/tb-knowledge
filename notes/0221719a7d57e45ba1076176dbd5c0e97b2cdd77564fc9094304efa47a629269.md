# EWS and Graph share a read-only header block and explicit header-name constants

The patch moves HeaderBlock into protocol_shared and uses its rfc5322_header names in both EWS and Graph extraction. The vector-backed block exposes checked indexed name/value access and serializes CRLF header lines followed by a blank line. The exact shared implementation survives unchanged at the accepted revision. Constants are the landed local choice; this does not establish a global naming rule, performance benefit or general value-validation guarantee.

Scope: Exchange header block bridge.
