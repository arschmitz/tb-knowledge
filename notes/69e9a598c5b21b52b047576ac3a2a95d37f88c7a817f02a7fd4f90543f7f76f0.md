# Validate the cipher value separately from the protection mode

RNP protection information supplies separate mode, cipher and integrity values. Bug 1943620 changed the null comparison to cipher rather than mode. At the fixed accepted source, invalid integrity suppresses decoded data and marks decryption failure; a null or forbidden cipher marks UNKNOWN_ALGO and does not reach DECRYPTION_OKAY. Keep those distinct values and branches in reviews of this protection path. Static source inspection does not prove cryptographic safety or exercise malformed inputs. Complete Bugzilla and historical Phabricator timeline/inline discussion remains pending.

Scope: mail / extensions.
