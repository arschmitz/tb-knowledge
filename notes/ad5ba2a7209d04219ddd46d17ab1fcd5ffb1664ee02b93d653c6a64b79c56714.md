# Accepted ADR0001 records ongoing transition direction with coexistence and exceptions

ADR0001 is marked Accepted and records pre-existing technology transitions because both old and new approaches remain in the codebase. Its choices retain qualifiers: HTML may leave required XUL features, XPCOM remains where needed, and modules or moz-src are preferences in their stated scopes. Rollout is mostly improvement while touching code, with possible urgency from upstream changes and ESR uplift risk. The ADR process records a decision reached through relevant discussion and rough consensus. This is accepted document evidence, not proof of completed migration or permission to rewrite unrelated code or instructions. No documentation build ran.

Scope: Thunderbird architecture decision records and technology guidance.
