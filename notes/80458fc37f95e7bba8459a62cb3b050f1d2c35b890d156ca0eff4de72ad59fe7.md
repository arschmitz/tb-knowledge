# Firefox native HTML date input calendar support

Firefox web-exposed locales can reflect OS regional/app preferences or fingerprinting protections. They affect field order, month names, digits, RTL direction, first day of week, and similar presentation—not the calendar system or stored date. Sites needing Hebrew, Islamic, Persian, or another calendar need their own control and conversion logic. [Task 1]

Scope: calendar.
