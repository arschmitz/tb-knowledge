# Commands Build/Run checkout, cache provenance, and freshness status

`build-freshness.mjs` tracks Firefox/Thunderbird revisions, tracked/untracked content, resolved configuration, platform/architecture, and non-empty installed output per `working` or `review` checkout. It invalidates before build and only publishes current after successful unchanged inputs; old builds are `unknown`. [Task 2]

Scope: console.
