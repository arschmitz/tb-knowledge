# Commands sprint membership controls and view isolation

`commands/graph/client/meta-board-visibility.js` centralizes `isClosedBoardBug(card)` and `isAbandonedBoardPatch(patch)`. Closed means `isOpen === false`, `resolved|verified|closed`, or a non-`---` resolution; abandoned checks both `status` and `statusName` for `/abandon/i`. [Task 2]
