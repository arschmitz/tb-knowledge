# Commands patch-review conflict recovery, single-patch fallback, and capacity status

A saved capacity failure may have `status: "error"`, the real capacity error, and stale `message: "Command completed"`. Map the tray to `Failed`; a guided retry must validate checkout/hash, preserve guidance, and clear `error`, `restored`, `interrupted`, and `recoveryFailed`. Keep rejected action errors separate from the task error. [Task 3]

Scope: console.
