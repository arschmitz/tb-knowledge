# Keep crypto-icon test expectations tied to current display policy

Entry 370 made a file-opened S/MIME failure assert a visible mismatch icon in 2024. That assertion is historical. A later accepted change, f30b53fb950 (Bug 1836262), hides bad or unknown signature icons for unencrypted messages while retaining warnings when encryption is present. Current setMessageCryptoBox tests encryptedState and the signed-state list, maps notok to mismatch, sets a localized icon alt text when shown, and removes alt/src/l10n data when hidden. Follow this newer conditional policy when writing tests. Only the relevant later source diff was read; its Bugzilla/review and runtime/accessibility behavior were not studied.

Scope: Current crypto icon policy.
