# General preferences removes the update observer only in updater builds

AUTO_UPDATE_CHANGED_TOPIC registration occurs inside AppConstants.MOZ_UPDATER. The owned fix adds the same condition to its removal during destroy. The window unload listener and mailnews.tags. observer have separate lifetimes and remain outside that condition. This prevents trying to remove that update observer in builds where it was never added; it does not establish repeated-destroy safety, recover from partial initialization or fix unrelated rebranding/shutdown errors. No disabled-updater build or runtime test ran.

Scope: Preferences observer lifetime.
