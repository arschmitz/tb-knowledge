# Allow a deliberate override for hostname-mismatch certificates

The July 2024 fix makes certificate notifications clickable for hostname mismatch too. A click can open the exception dialog after showing the precise mismatch warning. This replaced the May 2024 block: other invalid certificate classes can also permit interception, and the block prevented users of some servers from adding a needed override.

Scope: mail certificate alerts.
