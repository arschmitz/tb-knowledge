# Tie Thunderbird sandbox preference values to their platform and revision

The public preference patch guards its values with each platform and MOZ_SANDBOX. Historical content levels are Windows8, macOS3 and Linux4; fixed accepted source has7,3 and 5. The numbers alone do not define their security effect or prove the effective policy of a running process. The source points to Firefox for their meaning, which was not inspected. Required discussion is restricted/unread; no runtime or security validation.

Scope: Platform sandbox preference scope.
