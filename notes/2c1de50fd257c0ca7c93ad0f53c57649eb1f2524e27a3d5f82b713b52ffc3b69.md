# Paint the iMIP menu button background on its parent control

Entry 981 moves the layered background to the parent message-header toolbarbutton. Its menu-button and dropmarker children keep border color, rather than each painting another copy of the background. Current CSS retains that structure and distinguishes ordinary pressed buttons, menu buttons and the More control. The markup identifies the real toolbarbutton-menu-button consumer. Keep styling scoped to the relevant control parts when extending this family. No live hover, pressed, RTL, contrast or dropmarker render was checked.

Scope: calendar/base/themes.
