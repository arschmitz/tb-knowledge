# A pagination fixture selects callback positions that its count-only assertion does not distinguish

The returnMessageListId fixture starts its counter at 1, increments it before looking up keys 6 and 55, and therefore selects callback positions 5 and 54. Its two-message assertion cannot distinguish those positions from the described sixth and fifty-fifth messages. The mismatch persists in the inspected accepted source. This is a static fixture and assertion-scope finding, without a reproduced message-order error or CI failure.

Scope: WebExtension pagination test assertions.
