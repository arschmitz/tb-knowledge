# Selection preference tests compare the quoted result and whole-message fallback

The preference test reads the composed blockquote text after opening a reply. It checks whole-message fallback when a one-word selection is disallowed, when selection quoting is disabled, and when the selected text lacks a required character. It also checks accepted single-word, multi-word and required-character selections. The assertions check the quoted text; they do not directly verify native IPC serialization or all Unicode word boundaries.

Scope: Compose selection-quoting browser-test contracts.
