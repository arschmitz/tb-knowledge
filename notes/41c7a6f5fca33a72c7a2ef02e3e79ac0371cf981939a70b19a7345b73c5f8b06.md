# Month day headings use the selected-day tab stop and explicit keyboard activation

The month day heading has role button and handles Space and Enter explicitly. The selected box gives its heading tabindex0; other headings use minus1. Keyboard activation selects the heading date when present, then calls the calendar view controller's createNewEvent. The pointer handlers remain distinct: ordinary click clears item selection and double-click creates an event. This is an exact source contract, not a native button or rendered accessibility result.

Scope: Calendar month and multiweek view day headings.
