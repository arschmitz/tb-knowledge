# Match native option cleanup to its allocation and destructor

The accepted display-stream bridge creates MimeDisplayOptions with new but still uses PR_Free(msd->options) on text-converter failure and URL-option parse failure. Its destructor frees option-owned strings, and other checked failure/normal paths use delete. Recommend auditing allocation family and owned fields when extending this resource-leak fix. This is a static cleanup concern; no allocator mismatch, crash or leaked child allocation was reproduced. This is source and test-code inspection. Complete Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: mailnews/mime/src.
