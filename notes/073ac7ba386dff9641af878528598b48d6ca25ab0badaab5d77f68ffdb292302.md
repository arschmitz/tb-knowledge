# Collapsed checkbox rows combine their own choice with descendant caches

A collapsed checkbox group is checked only when its own checked property and every checkable descendant are checked. A partial combination is indeterminate. Expanded groups and leaves show only their own literal choice. The delegated toggle path cascades downward only while collapsed, updates all/some/none descendant caches and ripples changed aggregates upward. Direct tests cover state/cache transitions in dense fixtures; they do not establish native focus, announcements or arbitrary external cache invalidation.

Scope: Checkbox tree collapsed subtree choice aggregation.
