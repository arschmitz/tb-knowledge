# Calendar new-item setup checks target visibility before the synthetic click

The owned helper sets its dialog wait first, yields to an idle callback, scrolls a supplied target and asserts visibility before the synthetic double-click. Accepted source keeps that sequence with later size and click-offset changes. The assertion diagnoses an invisible target; an idle callback and zero-delay yields do not prove a condition has settled or that the intermittent failure is fixed.

Scope: calendar-new-item-helper-visibility-diagnostic.
