# Use actor queries for scroll measurements and messages for page scroll commands

MessageScrollParent.getSize uses sendQuery to obtain scroll coordinates, while scrollByPages sends an asynchronous command and exposes no completion promise. The child resolves getSize to an object, scrollByPages to null and rejects an unknown command. The initial actor and JSDoc follow-up were both backed out exactly; later 1841 context and the accepted source restore this protocol. Do not treat the two backouts as rejection of this architecture or as proof of a failure cause. These are fixed accepted source and test-code observations. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint, live accessibility or crypto/TLS validation ran.

Scope: mail/actors/MessageScrollParent.sys.mjs.
