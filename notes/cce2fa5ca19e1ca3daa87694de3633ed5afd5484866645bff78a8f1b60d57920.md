# The hosted rights page keeps about:rights callers and sets target principal metadata separately

The landing replaces the packaged rights page with an HTTPS target while keeping existing about:rights callers. Its redirector sets resultPrincipalURI for targets whose URI chain lacks URI_IS_UI_RESOURCE before creating the channel. It then preserves the original about URI and, for safe-for-untrusted entries, sets a content-principal owner from that about URI. The rights mapping also requires a child load. These are exact comm assignments and callers; upstream principal/channel enforcement and hosted-page rendering are unverified.

Scope: Thunderbird about:rights redirect.
