# Marionette job enablement and coverage data need separate checks

The initial Bug 1898400 patch restored jobs, but the bug reopened because Thunderbird coverage settings were omitted. A later accepted D212513 patch added the missing config. Current marionette.yml includes a Thunderbird extra config on platform branches. When changing CI test jobs, verify that the job runs and that its coverage data is collected for coverage builds; a green job alone does not establish coverage integration.
