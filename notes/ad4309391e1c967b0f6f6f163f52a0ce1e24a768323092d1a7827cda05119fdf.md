# CalDAV multiget yields after changed items are added, not after every response

The accepted CalDavMultigetSyncHandler increments its yield counter only after awaiting addTargetCalendarItem for a changed or new item. Every ten such additions it waits for a zero-delay timer and resets the counter. Unchanged ETags skip that work, and bad items are logged/continued. The network batch pref and this CPU/event-loop yield count are separate controls; ten is not a general CalDAV batching rule. This is static source and test-code inspection.
