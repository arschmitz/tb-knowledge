# The policy module exports resolved definitions and resets tests to the same resolved object

The module imports JSON and resolves its supported local definitions once before exporting schema. It throws for an unknown or cyclic definition name and merges sibling keywords after the resolved object. modifySchemaForTests accepts a supplied object directly; reset restores resolvedSchema rather than cloning or resolving it again. Keep that local inliner/reset contract separate from the upstream policy validator and from a fresh policy or background-task test.

Scope: Enterprise policy schema loading.
