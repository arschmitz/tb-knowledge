# Tie the policy notice to active policy state and measured sticky height

The preferences notice appears when Services.policies is ACTIVE and links to about:policies. Its wording indicates management rather than claiming every preference is disabled. The selected code measures the actual sticky container, adds focus-rectangle space and applies scroll padding to main content. Fixed source retains this condition and measurement; rendered geometry, policy setup and accessibility were not tested.

Scope: Tie the policy notice to active policy state and measured sticky height.
