# Guarding an unsigned countdown does not prevent its final wrap

Convert_whitespace now guards an initially zero counter before while(number_of_nbsp--) or while(number_of_space--). A positive counter still reaches a final zero post-decrement condition, which wraps the unsigned value after the last body execution. The counters are not used afterward in this helper. Recommend an explicit nonzero condition with a decrement in the body when the final counter value matters. Do not describe this historical patch as removing every unsigned wrap or as proof of an infinite loop. This is source and test-code inspection. Complete Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: mailnews/mime/src.
