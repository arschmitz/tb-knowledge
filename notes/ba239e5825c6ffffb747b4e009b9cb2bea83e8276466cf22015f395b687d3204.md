# Enabling Google PKCE retains the separately configured client secret

The patch adds usePKCE:true to the Google issuer without removing its client secret. In the accepted consumer, those settings are copied independently: PKCE supplies the challenge and authorization-code verifier, while a non-null configured secret is still sent to the token endpoint. Review records the author's failed-without-secret report, not an independently verified provider requirement. This is a scoped provider decision; no authentication or security guarantee was tested.

Scope: Google OAuth provider settings.
