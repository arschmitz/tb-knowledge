# Thunderbird restored-window notification precedes completed tab loads

atStartupRestoreTabs calls loadExtraTabs and emits mail-tabs-session-restored and sessionstore-windows-restored while its source explicitly says tabs have not finished loading. The normal startup path also emits the restored-window topic before account verification and post-account UI. MailGlue guards repeated restored-window handling before telemetry and ExtensionsUI initialization. Treat this as the inspected startup phase, not a promise that all tab loads or upstream policy work have completed.

Scope: Thunderbird startup notification ordering.
