# Use a fresh OpenPGP handler factory while keeping stream callback ownership explicit

PgpMimeHandler.newHandler directly returns a new Handler instance. The handler chooses its content-type delegate, binds data and stop callbacks to that delegate, and deletes those callback references after stop handling. Preserve that per-handler lifetime and delegate binding when changing the factory. The original wrapper-constructor simplification is historical context; this selected current source does not prove every MIME streaming or shutdown path. Complete Bugzilla and historical Phabricator timeline/inline discussion remains pending.

Scope: mail / extensions.
