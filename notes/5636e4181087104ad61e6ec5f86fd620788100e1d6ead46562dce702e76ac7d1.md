# Commands fork-aware graph rebase replay

Fork planning must derive `replayParents` from commit parents and replay every child from its rewritten parent. Update aliases and sibling refs. `chooseRebaseStackCandidate`, `rebaseCommitUnlocked`, and conflict continuation must accept supported single-parent forks; reject merge commits or missing/unplanned parents explicitly.
