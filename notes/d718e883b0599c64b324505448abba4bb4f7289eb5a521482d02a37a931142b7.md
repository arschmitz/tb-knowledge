# Sandbox issuer tests check frozen client IDs without checking toggle-back isolation

The two added issuer-level tests assert Object.isFrozen and the expected production or sandbox client ID. The sandbox task then clears the preference without another lookup. These assertions do not check object identity, endpoint values, production isolation after toggling back or authentication. Keep those direct assertions separate from the broader repair intent and historical Works report.

Scope: OAuth provider regression-test scope.
