# The policy comparison allows exact diff lines and rejects stale allowances

The browser test serializes policy methods with Function.toString and compares their source. Its allowed differences are exact arrays of generated diff lines, including retained context. The checker counts both unexpected inserted lines and removed stale allowance lines; obsolete entries also fail. This is a source-consistency check, not execution of the policy callbacks or proof of platform parity. The accepted checker is generalized for later lists, while the original landing compares policy implementations only.

Scope: Enterprise policy comparison browser test.
