# Index only cards with a usable remote href during reconciliation

924 filters out cards without _href before constructing the full-fetch comparison map. The newer accepted _getCardsByHref helper normalizes the href, keeps only a truthy result and builds a fresh map for each comparison. Full fetch removes known remote hrefs it sees and deletes only the remaining map entries. Cards without a usable remote href are outside that remote-presence deletion set. Do not infer that this class never deletes or uploads those cards through another path.

Scope: CardDAV remote identity reconciliation.
