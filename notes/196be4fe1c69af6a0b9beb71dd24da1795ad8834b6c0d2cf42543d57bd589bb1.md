# Remove temporary Matrix membership listeners on both success and rejection

searchForVerificationRequests waits for the current user’s Join or Leave event when invited and removes its RoomMember.membership listener in finally. The direct-verification member wait likewise clears its timeout and listener in finally. These are scoped cleanup patterns to preserve across success, rejection and timeout paths; they do not prove every account listener is removed on shutdown. This is source and test inspection only.
