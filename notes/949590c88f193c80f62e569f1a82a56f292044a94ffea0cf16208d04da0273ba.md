# Selection quoting depends on the displayed message and context-menu override

The accepted compose dispatcher gets selected message URIs from the folder/synthetic view, or uses gMessageURI for a standalone message. It asks the MailMessage actor for quoting selection only when context-menu selection is not overridden, a message browser exists and the preference allows it. Preserve this selection ownership when changing compose dispatch. It was not introduced by the wrapper removals, and no actor or quoting interaction ran. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: compose selection ownership.
