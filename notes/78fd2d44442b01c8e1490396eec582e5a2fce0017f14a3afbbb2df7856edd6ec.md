# Keep the QR wizard index private and expose bounded navigation

QrCodeWizard holds private codes and a zero-based step, clones its template once on connection and resets the step when initialized. next/back return booleans at the ends; the pane uses those booleans to choose Intro or Summary. Captions expose one-based progress. An empty initialization has total zero and isLastStep true in tests, and the pane returns to Intro before displaying codes. Empty next() and an empty image source were not validated, so the total-zero guard remains a caller requirement rather than a claim of universal widget safety.

Scope: mail/components/preferences.
