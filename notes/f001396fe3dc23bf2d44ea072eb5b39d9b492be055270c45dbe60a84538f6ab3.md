# The Email success view retains its branding-header request

This visibility change removes branding requests from six intermediate subviews. EmailAddedSuccess still calls super.showBrandingHeader after connecting its template, in both the owned and accepted source. AccountHubStep forwards that request to AccountHubHeader, which clears the branding region's hidden property. The initial Email and Address Book option views also retain their requests. Read this as specific retained callers rather than a first-subview-only rule. The new visibility tests do not directly cover the Email success request, and no rendering or accessibility check ran here.

Scope: Account Hub branding.
