# Thunderbird browser chunking removes the manifest-loader override and enables harness chunking together

The change removes the explicit null test-manifest-loader override and enables mozharness.chunked for the Thunderbird browser test. The local kind passes this configuration through its test loader and imported Gecko transforms. These are the two owned configuration changes; they do not by themselves verify the imported transform default, generated chunk membership or a successful CI run. Current platform chunk counts belong to other phases.

Scope: Thunderbird browser-test task configuration.
