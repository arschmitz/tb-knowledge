# Gate notification startup in MailGlue

The initial May 2024 notification scaffold starts from MailGlue only when mail.inappnotifications.enabled is true. The next storage commit makes init asynchronous and catches its rejection at the startup call site. Treat the first scaffold as an incremental design, not the complete notification system.
