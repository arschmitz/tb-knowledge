# OTR notification boxes are selected on demand and captured per operation

OTRUI.globalBox resolves the most recent mail:3pane window and its chatHandler notification box instead of storing a singleton DOM field. The handler caches the box only on that window. Notification methods capture one box locally for their operation, including append awaits; the local name keeps the operation on its selected box without specifying garbage-collection timing. The getter assumes a suitable mail window exists.

Scope: OTR window ownership.
