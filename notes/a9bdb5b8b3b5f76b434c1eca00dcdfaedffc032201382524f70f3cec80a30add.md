# Empty-store regression assertions cover both supported store backends

test_asyncScan loops over one message, two messages and an empty message array, then compares collected raw messages without assuming backend ordering. withStore runs these tasks for mbox and maildir. PromiseStoreScanListener resolves on a successful onStopScan and rejects on another status. These tests cover result contents and completion status; they do not separately assert every callback order, listener lifetime or dispatch failure. This is static source and test-code inspection. Complete Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.
