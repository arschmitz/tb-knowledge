# Resolve the QR OAuth summary through the same default outgoing-server fallback

getAccountOAuthUsage resolves an explicit smtpServerKey or outgoingServer.defaultServer, just as eligibility and serialization do. It returns independent incoming and outgoing OAuth booleans. An empty key means the default server, rather than no SMTP server. Earlier scoped QR eligibility and serialization notes are reused; this lesson covers the OAuth summary consumer added by the assigned fix. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: QR export OAuth summary fallback.
