# The badge contrast fallback has lower selector specificity than its variants

This component puts explicit color/background declarations on .badge.beta, .badge.experimental, .badge.alpha and .badge.inverted, with another class for inverted subtypes. Its final prefers-contrast declarations stay on the less specific .badge selector. Under ordinary author-selector specificity, that branch does not replace the explicit variant colors. The original and fixed accepted component blocks match exactly. The Badge story shows the variants in two color-scheme contexts but has no contrast assertion. This is a bounded cascade finding; intended design coverage, forced-color adjustments and rendered contrast remain unverified.

Scope: Shared badge variant cascade.
