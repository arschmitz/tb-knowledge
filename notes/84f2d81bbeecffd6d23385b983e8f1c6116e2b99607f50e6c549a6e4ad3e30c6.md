# The Address Book opener adds a timer turn before its separate card-list readiness wait

openAddressBookWindow now waits one timer turn inside its onLoad callback before resolving the content window, then still waits for cards-list readiness. The actual new-tab producer already delays onLoad after about-addressbook-ready, while the existing-tab route may call it directly or from a ready event. Keep these callback and list-ready boundaries separate; the new turn is not a general synchronization or CardDAV completion guarantee. Required primary comment 58 remains unavailable, so the finding has zero full-audit credit.

Scope: Address-book browser test callback completion.
