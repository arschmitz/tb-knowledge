# Encrypted attachment deletion checks stored-message safety after reading the requested parts

Attachment listing and retrieval parse a transient decrypted view, while AttachmentInfo deletion rewrites the stored message. deleteAttachments reads and validates requested parts, then rejects when the processor retains hasEncryptedParts before it calls the rewrite helper. The getter depends on encryption state established during those reads; parsing the decrypted part with checkForEncryption:false preserves that state, and the decryptor can correct false positives. Keep the check before the mutation boundary and preserve earlier validation/decryption errors. The schema requires at least one requested part and documents encrypted deletion as unsupported. This does not authorize modifying the decrypted temporary tree or imply every invalid encrypted request reaches the same final error.

Scope: mail/extensions/messages/deleteAttachments.
