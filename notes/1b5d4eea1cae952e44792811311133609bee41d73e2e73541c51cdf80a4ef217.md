# Update insecure-connection warnings before preserving custom ports

Incoming and outgoing form adjustments toggle warning when socketType is plain before they return early for a custom port. The warning therefore follows security selection even when port numbers should not be overwritten. Keep the warning update ahead of that early return; port preservation and user-visible risk information are separate state changes. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending.
