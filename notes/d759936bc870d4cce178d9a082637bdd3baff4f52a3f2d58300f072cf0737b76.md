# Candidate ARM artifact maps keep path, tool and filename names separate

The candidate manifest adds both ARM shippable platforms to its path, tool and filename roles. Linux uses linux-aarch64 in candidate paths and linux64-aarch64 for tool/filename substitutions. The checksum manifest also accepts the plain ARM platform keys. Actual release-type selectors choose these candidate maps for beta/release/ESR. The source connects names to declarative consumers; it does not establish a successful taskgraph or artifact publication.

Scope: Thunderbird ARM candidate artifact naming.
