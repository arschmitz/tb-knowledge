# Unlock the signature authority even when editing a subkey

Expiry editing must distinguish keys to unlock from keys whose expiry changes. A subkey expiry signature needs the primary secret key unlocked, plus the selected subkey when applicable. In D212003, automatic passphrase mode had already unlocked the primary while key details were open and masked the missing unlock. Test protected and unprotected keys, plus cached and cold unlock states when changing this path. Current RNP.changeKeyExpiration centralizes unlock/edit sets and releases trackers in finally. Validation here is source inspection and reported historical manual checks.

Scope: OpenPGP expiry.
