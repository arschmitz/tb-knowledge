# Dark-reader background removal checks image, color and shorthand channels

The owned adapter change includes backgroundImage in its entry check, strips non-none non-gradient images, removes shorthand when an unsuitable background color is removed, and checks both shorthand and image fields in its final gradient branch. The new fixture uses yellow fallback shorthands with an image or gradient. Its assertions inspect empty inline properties and the body attribute; they do not establish visual contrast, image luminance or a change to stored MIME source.

Scope: Loaded-message dark mode style sanitization.
