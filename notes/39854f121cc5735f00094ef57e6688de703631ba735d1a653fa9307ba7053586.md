# Keep Matrix verification enums aligned across export and account options

The owned Matrix SDK wrapper exports VerificationMethod from matrix-sdk/types, and MatrixAccount imports that symbol lazily and selects VerificationMethod.Sas in its client options. The historical update removes the old MatrixCrypto.verificationMethods.SAS route and packages types.js. Preserve the exact SDK export name, public enum member spelling and owned consumer together when moving this boundary. This is an owned integration contract. Imported SDK bodies do not supply Thunderbird style rules, and selecting the option does not prove that a live SAS verification succeeds. Static source/review checks only; no Matrix connection or crypto operation was tested.

Scope: chat/protocols/matrix.
