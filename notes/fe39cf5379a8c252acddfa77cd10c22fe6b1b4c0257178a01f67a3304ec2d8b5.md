# Keep Gtest suite and case names distinct in the Mbox family

Accepted native tests pair TestMsgMboxRead.cpp with TEST(TestMsgMboxRead, Basics), OddCases and Ambiguities, and TestMsgMboxWrite.cpp with its own suite and behavior cases. Shared data remains MboxTestData.cpp/.h. Scoped naming pattern: keep the Test-prefixed suite/file family distinct from reusable fixture data, and use a behavior case name that identifies what failed. This does not impose native naming on browser or xpcshell tests. This is source and test inspection only. Full Bugzilla and historical review discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran.

Scope: mailnews/base/test.
