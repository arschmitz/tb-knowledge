# Wait for a completion predicate in IMAP sync runnables

A condition-variable wait may wake spuriously. Keep waiting until the main-thread runnable marks completion; returning early leaves the runnable holding references to unwound IMAP-thread stack values and can crash when it runs later.

Scope: imap.
