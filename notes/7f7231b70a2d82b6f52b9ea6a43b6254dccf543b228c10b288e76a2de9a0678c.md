# The proxy test checks absent FTP preferences through their invalid type

The proxy address test replaces Preferences.has checks with direct comparisons of Services.prefs.getPrefType against Ci.nsIPrefBranch.PREF_INVALID for the two FTP preferences. This preserves an explicit type-based absence expectation after FTPProxy input. It is not a user-value-only predicate or a production policy change, and the test was not executed.

Scope: Enterprise proxy policy test preference absence checks.
