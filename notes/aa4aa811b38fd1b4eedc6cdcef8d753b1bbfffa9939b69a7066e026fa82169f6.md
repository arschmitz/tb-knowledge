# Name eligibility sets by their positive or negative membership meaning

In QR export, INCOMING_SERVER_TYPES lists supported protocol types, while UNSUPPORTED_AUTH_METHODS lists rejected authentication methods, including values that do not map to an export method. Callers use has() with the matching positive or negative condition. Manual naming proposal for comparable policy sets: make the accepted or rejected meaning clear in the constant name and keep the condition consistent. Uppercase underscore names are the local constants pattern; this example does not define every constant name in comm.

Scope: mail/qrexport/policy-set-naming.
