# Calendar footer width can overflow with inline margins

The AI review of D335830 at revision 0f681d525bd4142095c44bfd9cbf32ecb3cc10c4 reports that the footer's 100% inline size plus inline margins placed Save 9 pixels outside the production dialog, on opposite sides in left-to-right and right-to-left layouts. It reports that inline-size: auto passed the bounds checks in both directions. When changing this footer, check its bounds with margins in both directions. This is a reported local experiment, not independently confirmed evidence or an accepted general CSS convention; the record has no human feedback or landing outcome.

Scope: Calendar event dialog footer.
