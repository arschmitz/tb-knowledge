# A late MIME options check does not protect earlier options accesses

The callback dereferences options for decompose_init_count before it selects a charset. Removing options from the later override_charset condition therefore removes a redundant late check, rather than making null options safe. The selected setup allocates options before installing callbacks, and the multipart caller gates callback dispatch and propagates negative results. These inspected paths bound the precondition; they do not prove all callers or a reproduced crash.

Scope: Draft decomposition callback precondition.
