# Keep the at-most-one-alarm-action constraint when splitting a summary

The summary for addReminderImages combines clearing the container with adding reminder images. Its duplicate-action check and unsupported-action skip support the limit of at most one icon per action. For this function, split the actions if useful, but do not replace the limit with a promise of an icon for every action. This is static source evidence, not a repository-wide sentence template.
