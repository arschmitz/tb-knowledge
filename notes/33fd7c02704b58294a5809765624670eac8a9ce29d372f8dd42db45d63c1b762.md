# Destination-stream close failure takes precedence over the scan status

OnStopScan closes and clears mDestStream before promoting the scan status. Only a successful Close assigns rv = status; a close error remains the effective failure and prevents the success-only renames. The listener is still informed and final summary receives that result. Scoped failure-handling lesson: finishing a scan is insufficient if buffered output cannot be closed successfully. This is static source and test-code inspection at the frozen accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator timeline and inline discussion remain pending; a landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: mbox compaction output close status precedence.
