# The inspected Thunderbird Cookies schema declares fewer fields than its callback handles

At the fixed accepted source, the Cookies schema lists Allow, Block, Default, AcceptThirdParty, ExpireAtSessionEnd and Locked. The callback also contains AllowSession, Behavior, BehaviorPrivateBrowsing and RejectTracker branches. The inspected schema object does not declare those newer names. This is a static contract boundary; the paired Gecko engine and validator were not inspected, so runtime reachability or rejection is unproved.

Scope: Enterprise policy Cookies schema boundary.
