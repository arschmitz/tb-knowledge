# Initialize missing-recipient collections to their empty array shape

930 initializes gEmailsWithMissingKeys and gEmailsWithMissingCerts to [] instead of null. Their accepted consumers inspect length, and the collectors fill arrays. Preserve the empty collection shape when callers use array operations before the first check finishes. The plural names describe collections; no repository-wide ban on null sentinels is inferred.

Scope: Compose missing-key collection defaults.
