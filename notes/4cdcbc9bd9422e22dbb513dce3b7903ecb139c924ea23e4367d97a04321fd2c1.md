# Keep pre-open channel attributes outside an uninitialized pump

In D228378 the storage reviewer warns that callers set LoadGroup, owner and flags before AsyncOpen, while a pump may not yet be initialized. After acceptance with follow-up concerns, the author restores local getters/setters; guarded Cancel/Suspend proxying stays separate. The actual landing stores LoadGroup locally. Later review clarification notes that cited examples also keep their own LoadGroup. This is the scoped channel initialization contract, not a universal ban on proxy objects or proof that current pump internals fail. No channel or pump test ran.

Scope: EWS channel pre-open lifetime.
