# Keep spaces helper and manifest names aligned after splitting tests

The spaces tests load head_spaces.js through gTestPath and separate cross-extension cases into browser_ext_spaces_multiple.js. At accepted source, browser6.toml registers both files and includes the shared helper. Its comment asks new tests to use the manifest containing their alphabetical neighbours. Recommend retaining this local filename family and manifest placement; the historical browser.ini is no longer the current registration file. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run.

Scope: mail/components/extensions.
