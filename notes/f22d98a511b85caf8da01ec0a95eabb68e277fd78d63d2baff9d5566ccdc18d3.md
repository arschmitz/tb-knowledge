# CalDAV removes its uncalled oauthConnect prompt wrapper without replacing OAuth

The patch deletes the callback wrapper and its queued async-prompt listener. A literal search over calendar paths finds only that declaration before removal and no occurrence at the accepted revision. The accepted calendar still creates a CalDavSession using stored username or calendar id. The six-year disuse claim is attributed historical context; the change supplies no new authentication path, dynamic-call proof or runtime safety result. Do not generalize this local removal into a ban on async prompting or OAuth.

Scope: Calendar CalDAV local dead-code boundary.
