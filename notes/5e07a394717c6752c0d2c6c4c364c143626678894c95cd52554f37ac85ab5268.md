# Keep reserved mailbox-name failures distinct from hostname rescue

The current hostname policy is already recorded in lesson 43b9d496. A narrower boundary remains useful: createKeyedServer immediately rejects Local Folders or smart mailboxes with type other than none, and also rejects their percent-encoded spellings. It substitutes <key>.invalid only for ordinary hostnames that fail domain/URL checks. CreateIncomingServer validates before LoadAccounts and unique-key allocation. Do not describe rescue as accepting every invalid reserved name.

Scope: mailnews/base/src.
