# Background-update settings and startup scheduling have separate comm-side gates

Entry2137 adds a MailGlue task gated by MOZ_UPDATE_AGENT, with MOZ_UPDATER and updateDisabledForTesting checks before calling maybeScheduleBackgroundUpdateTask. The startup dispatcher also skips shutdown. Settings visibility separately requires per-installation support, allowed policy, unpackaged app and an unlocked background setting. The checkbox is disabled when auto updates are off; its initial read performs the rollout transition before reading disk. Fixed0b93 retains these comm-side gates. They do not prove the OS task was scheduled or that an update succeeded: Gecko scheduler/config implementations and native behavior were not inspected. No test/build ran.

Scope: Thunderbird background update integration.
