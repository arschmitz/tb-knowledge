# Treat the notification setup delays as unresolved workarounds

1099 and 1115 add a one-second delay after browserLoaded and promiseFocus with a local mozilla/no-arbitrary-setTimeout suppression. Both remain in the accepted tests. The comment says the tests misbehave when started immediately, but no readiness condition or precise cause is identified. Preserve the recorded limitation and seek an observable readiness condition when revisiting it; do not turn the delay into a general test-style rule or claim it proves the root cause.

Scope: Notification button test startup workaround.
