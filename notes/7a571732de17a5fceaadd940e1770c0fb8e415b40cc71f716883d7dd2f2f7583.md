# Preserve the XPCOM stream-read size and completion limits

1108's shared reader checks the u64 Available size fits Read's u32, allocates a byte buffer, and returns only bytes_read. Its TODO says it assumes all needed data is already available and may be unsuitable for several messages. The accepted protocol_shared reader still performs one read and carries that TODO. Do not present this helper as a drain-until-EOF loop or apply it to incremental streams without changing that contract.

Scope: XPCOM byte-stream one-read limit.
