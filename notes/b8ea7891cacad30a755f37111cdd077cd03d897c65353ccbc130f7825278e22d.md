# Preserve zero when reading a stored notification seed

Current seed lookup checks Object.hasOwn before returning the stored number. Seed0 is valid and must not cause rerandomization through || or truthiness. A stable per-ID seed and saveSoon keep eligibility stable across repeated reads and profile reloads. This is a supported data/defaulting pattern in notification storage, not a generic ban on ||.

Scope: notifications.
