# Message-display and address-book tab status requires a nonblank complete document

For messageDisplay and addressBook, Tab.status requires both a location other than about:blank and readyState complete before returning complete. Other tab types keep their own branches, including web-progress and message-pane visibility checks. This getter contract is separate from a particular load event, tabs.onUpdated delivery or a live document-readiness result.

Scope: Extension tab loading status.
