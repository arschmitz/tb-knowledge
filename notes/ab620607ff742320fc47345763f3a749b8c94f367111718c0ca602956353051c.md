# Advanced Configuration alignment remains coupled to the shared footer padding

This landing moves the Manual and Exchange Advanced Configuration buttons outside their forms and uses logical margins for alignment. The rule documents its dependence on the footer's2px block padding and 10px inline padding; the fixed comparison footer still declares those values. The source establishes this local coupling, not measured alignment, a general fixed-offset policy or a guarantee after future footer changes.

Scope: Account Hub advanced button and dialog footer CSS.
