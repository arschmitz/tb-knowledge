# Compose service existence does not guarantee a registered compose docshell

Content policy resolves the root docshell before asking the named Compose service for its compose object. GetMsgComposeForDocShell may fail for an absent weak-map entry; the caller deliberately returns a nullable reference for that case. Registry lookup resolves the weak compose reference into an owning result, and teardown unregisters the docshell with its own service guard. Keep service availability, weak registration and object/temporary-file lifetimes separate. No runtime test.

Scope: compose-docshell-service-contract.
