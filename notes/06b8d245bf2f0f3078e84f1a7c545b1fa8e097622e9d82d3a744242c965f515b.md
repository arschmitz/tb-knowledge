# Two Account Manager pages stop forcing an outer inline scroll container

The E2E and SMTP pages remove overflow:auto from their outer flex vbox while retaining the inner containerBox. Both load accountManage.css, which supplies their shared container sizing and padding. The patch changes those two inline scroll declarations; no new replacement scroller is introduced. Static markup does not establish scrollbar or rendered sizing results.

Scope: Account Manager E2E and SMTP page containers.
