# Pair LiveView listener attachment with identity-aware cleanup

LiveView supports one listener. The current adapter registers itself in its constructor and clears only itself when detached from a tree, then drops its view and row caches. ClearListener refuses to remove a different current listener. Preserve that identity check when cleanup may occur after replacement. It protects ownership; it does not permit multiple simultaneous listeners. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Panorama listener ownership.
