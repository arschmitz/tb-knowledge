# Queued edits must distinguish unchanged calendar selection from a move

The D332891 verification record at revision dab61204576552c4e7a6bce2f010ccb2b87047c1 reports that a queued title-only draft could reverse an earlier calendar move because its unchanged calendarId was treated as an explicit move. The reported controlled execution produced A to B, then B to A; an explicit-B control preserved B. For this Save path, compare draft.calendarId with baseline.calendarId and preserve the reloaded calendar when they match. Explicit calendar changes remain move requests. The record reports a local amendment and queued title/occurrence tests with Undo, but the changes were unstaged and uncommitted. No Thunderbird runtime or xpcshell test ran; this is an AI-reported finding and local validation, not a confirmed landing or general convention.
