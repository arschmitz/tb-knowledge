# Chat unload abandons the account-added wait instead of continuing OTR setup

With no chat account, _initOTR waits for account-added. Success removes the account observer and unload listener, then resolves. Unload removes the account observer and leaves the promise unsettled, so that wait does not continue into OTRUI.init for the closed window. This does not cover the preceding prpl-init wait or every chat startup resource.

Scope: Chat startup completion and unload.
