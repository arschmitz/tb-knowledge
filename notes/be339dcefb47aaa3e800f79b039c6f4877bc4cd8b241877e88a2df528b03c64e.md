# Account cleanup and final account shutdown are different phases

Entry 1212 initially registers account cleanup at quit-application-granted and final teardown at profile-before-change as two shutdown blockers. The fixed accepted code has changed: quit-application-granted is an ordinary observer that invokes CleanupOnExit, and only profile-before-change has the account-manager blocker. Keep the current cleanup observer separate from the final blocker when reasoning about shutdown order. This is a historical/current source comparison, not an explanation of the later change; application shutdown and network cleanup were not exercised. Full bug and review discussion remains pending.

Scope: Account-manager shutdown phases.
