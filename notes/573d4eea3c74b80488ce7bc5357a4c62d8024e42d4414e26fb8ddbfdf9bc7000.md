# Account for the decoder final callback before freeing its state

The accepted decoder interface documents that MimeDecoderDestroy can call its output callback one last time when abort_p is false, while flushing buffered data. Tagged closure conversion does not remove that lifetime requirement. Keep the callback target alive through normal destruction and distinguish abort from final flush. This is an existing decoder contract checked alongside the signature migration, not a new 931 behavior or a measured buffering result.

Scope: MIME decoder final flush lifetime.
