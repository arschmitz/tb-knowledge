# Keep a viewed campaign version distinct from the campaign URL

Historical entry 496 changes the campaign version from 5 to 6 and the 115 spring URL to the 128 appeal. Its historical messenger consumer compares viewedEOY < currentEOY, skips the new-profile policy notice and automation, opens the page in an external browser and persists the viewed version. The version changes eligibility; changing only the URL would not show a new campaign to users who already saw that version. These old preferences have no match under current mail/mailnews; do not teach this as the current notification subsystem. Required Bug 1906695 and D215926 context remains pending.
