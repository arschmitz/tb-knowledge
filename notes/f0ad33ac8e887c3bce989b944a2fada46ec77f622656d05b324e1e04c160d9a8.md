# Check alternate invitation scheduling routes

The imported D320863 review records that processJobQueue() modified invitations and called checkAndSend() without the new adaptScheduleAgent() helper. Its proposed correction was to adapt immediately before modifyItem(). When reviewing scheduling changes, inspect each route that modifies and sends an invitation; coverage of other callers does not establish coverage of the Calendar Invitations dialog. The proposed regression uses an existing CalDAV invitation with forceEmailScheduling=true and checks SCHEDULE-AGENT=CLIENT plus one outgoing reply. This is a provisional historical review claim, not independently verified source or runtime evidence.

Scope: Calendar invitation scheduling.
