# Keep keyboard-test comments aligned with the key actually sent

The sidebar test comment says to press Enter, but the test sends the space character. Its recipient comment says first contact, while the asserted pill is danielle and daniel appears first in the expected list. Proposed manual review rule: name the key and selected fixture that the assertions actually exercise. Do not teach Enter support from this test. The parenthesized use-strict expression after the import is also not evidence of a preferred directive style.
