# Separate notification selection from visible presentation and persistence

Every new non-null manager candidate adds to its in-memory history, including website-opening types without notification UI. Visible candidates emit an event and record shown telemetry; non-UI candidates execute the call to action. This selection history is per manager and is not the persisted interactedWith list. Do not equate every counted candidate with a rendered window or a permanent daily limit across restarts. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: In-app notification selection state.
