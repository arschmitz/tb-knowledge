# Finish DB view teardown before the destroying-view listener in close

DBViewWrapper.close dissociates search and closes dbView before listener.onDestroyingView(false). Entry292 moved this callback because listeners can reenter teardown and otherwise see partially dismantled state. Current close preserves the order. Other rebuilding paths use onDestroyingView(true) with their own contract; do not mechanically reorder them.

Scope: DBViewWrapper lifetime.
