# The EWS signed-test recipient tuple includes an extra closing bracket

The owned and accepted test pass alice@openpgp.example> as the address inside a recipient tuple. MessageGenerator documents unwrapped address tuples and appends a closing angle bracket itself; its To setter calls that formatter. Thus the generated To header contains an extra closing bracket compared with the fixture's To header. Check this discrepancy when maintaining the fixed-header setup. The task has no direct To-header assertion, and no parser, delivery or signature failure was reproduced here. Preserve deliberately invalid fixtures when their purpose is documented; this note records the actual mismatch without editing the fixture.

Scope: EWS test fixture header review.
