# CalDAV multiget yields after attempted changed-item work

This corrects the older successful-add wording. Current multiget increments its yield counter after attempting the changed/new item add, including when its awaited promise rejects and is caught. Every ten attempts in that branch it yields and resets the counter. An unchanged ETag skips the branch only when there is no listener forcing refresh; this.listener also enters it. Keep this work counter separate from successful synchronization and from the network batch preference; ten is not a general CalDAV batching rule. This is a scoped correction supported by the actual accepted condition, catch and counter placement. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.
