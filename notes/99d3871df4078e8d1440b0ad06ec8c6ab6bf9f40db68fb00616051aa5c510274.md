# The first full attendee view suppresses overview visibility events and caps large lists

The follow-up caches full-mode state, keeps hide-row events and the more-than-three summary in the overview, and initially replaces a full list above 50 guests with a localized fallback. The dialog populates and clears both views and exposes an expand action above three attendees. Direct tests cover eight listed guests and the 60-guest fallback. Accepted source later removes that cap and renders asynchronously, so its eventual-count waits are a separate phase rather than proof that the first synchronous assertion completed.

Scope: Calendar attendee subview mode.
