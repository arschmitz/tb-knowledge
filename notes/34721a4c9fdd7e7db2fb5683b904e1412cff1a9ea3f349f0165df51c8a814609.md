# The shared context menu declares icon fill through its menuitem custom property

This landing moves the 20% currentColor mix from a navigation icon's direct fill declaration to --menuitem-icon-fill on menupopup. The accepted shared stylesheet retains the custom property, and the platform menu theme imports it. Stroke and active-indicator colors remain separate. This establishes the declared comm theme scope only; the unpaired toolkit consumer and actual platform rendering were not verified.

Scope: Thunderbird context menu theme.
