# Grouped Panorama insertion keeps unloaded child counts separate from loaded rows

A closed group that has never loaded children increments its sparse children length, invalidates its header and returns. A previously loaded group instead adds a concrete row and updates either visible-row notifications or the closed header. The tests distinguish these states with counts and expected message IDs. They were read statically, not run; the separate sorting comparator issue remains unresolved.

Scope: Panorama grouped live-view insertion.
