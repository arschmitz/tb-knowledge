# Reserve one byte for the IMAP cache probe terminator

The cache Peeker has a 101-byte buffer and caps copied bytes at sizeof peekBuf - 1 when the input count is at least the capacity. It then writes a terminating zero byte at the capped index. Its caller requests at most kPeekBufSize - 1 too, but the callback still defends its own bound. Recommend matching payload capacity and sentinel capacity at this borrowed-byte boundary.
