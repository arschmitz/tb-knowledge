# A Calendar alarm acknowledgement may be absent from CalDAV after a server strips `X-MOZ-LASTACK`

A Calendar alarm acknowledgement may be absent from CalDAV after a server strips `X-MOZ-LASTACK`. Preserve a local acknowledgement timestamp and choose the later local/server value. Remove only legacy bare ignored-alarm entries, and mark migration complete only when cleanup actually occurs; `calendar.alarms.showmissed` affects whether cleanup should run. This was refined through review and tested for online-then-offline dismissal.
