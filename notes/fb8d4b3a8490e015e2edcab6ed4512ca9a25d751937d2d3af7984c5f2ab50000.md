# Calendar view telemetry counts guarded initialization rather than every tab switch

The labeled view_initialized counter is incremented when a base Calendar view or task tree passes its initialization guard. Its seven labels identify view types; they do not measure viewing duration or every tab switch. The direct test compares the full expected label/count structure while revisiting views and switching tabs. Agenda activation uses a separate active-state condition and later gains an active-calendar guard, so do not extend the base/task flag guarantee to every agenda lifecycle or to unique-user counts. No telemetry or browser run was performed.

Scope: Calendar initialization telemetry.
