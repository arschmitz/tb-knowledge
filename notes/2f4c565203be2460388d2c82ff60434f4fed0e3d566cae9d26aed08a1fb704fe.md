# An ADR records a decision reached in discussion

Thunderbird’s ADR process says discussion comes first and the ADR reflects the resulting decision. The documented supporters are the people closest to the topic plus the relevant module owner. The template asks for source discussion links, context, the decision, consequences/risks, alternatives, security considerations and rollout plan. Keep links to the underlying reasoning; an ADR is not evidence that an unrelated suggestion was approved. The frozen accepted template now lives under docs/adr/records rather than its original landing path. The later move is comparison context and belongs to separate chronology coverage. Static source and test inspection only. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending-single-browser-reader.

Scope: Thunderbird architecture decision process.
