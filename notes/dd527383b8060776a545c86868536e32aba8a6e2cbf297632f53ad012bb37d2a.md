# Resolve startup thread work at the actual service boundary

In the 2024 collector design, account/cache access begins on the main thread, folder loading/discovery runs in background work, and DatabaseCore converts a MozPromise result to the JS startup Promise. Current startup is synchronous and gated in account manager initialization by MOZ_PANORAMA plus mail.panorama.enabled at startup. These are different designs. Do not reuse the old background readiness contract in a current caller. Static source and test-code inspection only. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator timeline and inline discussion remains pending. No runtime or live accessibility result is claimed.

Scope: Panorama startup threads.
