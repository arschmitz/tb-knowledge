# Prefer early returns and clear statement spacing

This is deliberate user-directed guidance for code writing, review, verification,
and repair, including examples and local experiments. It also applies to tb-tools
console work. Automatic learning must not change it.

## Guidance

- Always prefer early returns. Preserve behavior, execution order, cleanup, and
  the caller's contract. Use guard clauses to keep the remaining work out of a
  nested branch. Do not skip required work or cleanup.
- Prefer a blank line before a return.
- Prefer a blank line after a block of variable declarations.
- Prefer blank lines before and after control structures, such as loops,
  if statements, and switch statements. Keep directly related statements grouped
  when that makes the structure easier to read.
- Always separate complex multiline calls from other statements with blank lines.
  Do not add blank lines just inside block boundaries.

Check logical grouping separately from lint and formatting. Keep the existing
language, component, file-role, and generated or imported code exceptions.
The return, declaration, and control-structure spacing items are preferences.
Complex multiline call separation is an explicit requirement. Do not turn a
clear grouping alternative allowed by the control-structure exception into a
defect. Keep unrelated code outside the change.
