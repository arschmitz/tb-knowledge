# Declare preference labels and update the definition when the tracked list grows

The initial boolean-preference Glean migration omitted fixed labels, exceeding the historical dynamic-label limit and losing values. The later fix adds labels and splits calendar preferences into their own probe. Current code tells contributors to update metrics.yaml when adding a tracked preference and notes100labels per probe. Proposed manual rule: keep the tracked list, platform-dependent additions and schema labels together; do not infer unlimited capacity from labeled_string or from a passing common label test.

Scope: preference-telemetry.
