# Failed compaction must close the temporary output stream

Entry 1191 adds an explicit output-stream close on scan failure so the temporary safe-output stream can clean up its file. At the fixed accepted revision, MboxCompactor closes and clears mDestStream at OnStopScan before handling the final status. This is the stream-cleanup boundary of compaction, not proof of atomic mailbox/database replacement. The full historical change and abort test were read; filesystem cleanup was not reproduced. The cached review contains only a short concern excerpt, so its disposition is unknown.

Scope: Mbox compaction stream cleanup.
