# Reduce history publication overhead without filling coverage gaps

Scope: the Thunderbird history ingestion workflow in tb-tools. These are measured observations and locally tested changes, not a repository-wide rule or a new ingestion result.

The one-hour October 13, 2026 trial completed 70 fresh eligible audits, including source, available bug and review context, shared publication and remote verification. It performed 37 publication operations; 22 handled one entry. Successful publication service totaled 796.418 seconds and overlapped other work. That total cannot be subtracted from elapsed time as a guaranteed saving.

Replaying the same saved arrivals under two batching policies produced 36 groups with the prior adjacency rule and a 60-second flush, versus 17 groups with explicit selected entries and a 120-second flush. The replay holds arrival times fixed and assumes valid input. It omits real publishing, repairs and scheduling. Nineteen fewer groups multiplied by the measured 21.76-second median suggests 413.44 seconds less serialized publication work. This is a projection, not observed wall-clock savings; the final replay enqueue occurred 59 seconds later.

The locally tested selector groups at most ten already audited entries and flushes at five ready entries, 120 seconds, or near the run deadline. It can combine entries across gaps, but keeps an exact selectedIndices list. Publication and checkpoint checks still prevent unread neighbors from receiving coverage.

A pure input check now detects known citation, assignment and proposal-status errors before publication. Only its explicit IngestionInputError holds the immutable input for correction while unrelated jobs continue. Storage, checksum, unexpected validator, publication and verification failures still stop. A correction identifies the rejected immutable predecessor and retains exactly its entry selection. Reused private comparison context remains private and does not become new public proof. Exact Git evidence and remote verification remain later gates.

Validation: 57 focused tests passed, as did ESLint on the six affected repository files. Read-only compatibility replay accepted all 37 saved successful publication jobs. Two original failed groups were rejected early for proposal promotion and unsupported lesson proof. A historical path mismatch still requires the downstream Git check. The direct command entry point was checked through the temporary-directory symlink without opening a live store.

For the next authorized run, analysts can claim at most five related ready entries at a time; readers should join analysis when their assigned collection ends, and the parent can handle larger outliers and the earliest gap. This is an operational proposal with no measured speedup yet. Keep one Bugzilla reader, the existing navigation spacing, complete context requirements and one publication writer.

No fresh history ingestion, browser collection or live throughput trial ran during this improvement work. Commands changes are local and uncommitted. The study remains paused at detailed ledger entry 750 with source lessons through 2117; those two measures have different coverage requirements.
