# Use explicit RefPtr declarations in the concrete copy-listener callers

D225737 requested explicit RefPtr<CopyMessageStreamListener> streamListener declarations instead of auto with a deduced RefPtr at three copy callers. The final landing and pinned accepted callers retain that form. Use this as an accepted local consistency pattern for this concrete helper. The separate ownership discussion was unresolved; this is not a rule against auto elsewhere or proof of a raw pointer lifetime contract. Static source and full review inspection only.

Scope: mailnews copy-listener construction.
