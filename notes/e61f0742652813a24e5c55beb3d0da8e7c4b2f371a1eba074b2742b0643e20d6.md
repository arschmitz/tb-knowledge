# The tab focus override is scoped to the currently selected tab

The tabmail stylesheet sets -moz-user-focus:ignore only in the selected-tab branch. The historical selector checks selected=true; the accepted selector later checks presence of selected. Keep this local focus override separate from switching tabs, header focus and blanket tab-focus suppression. The shared native focus behavior and keyboard/drag/accessibility outcomes were not executed here.

Scope: tabmail-selected-tab-user-focus-scope.
