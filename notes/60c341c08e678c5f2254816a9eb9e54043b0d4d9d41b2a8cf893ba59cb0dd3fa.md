# Calendar view coverage directly checks marker state and localized week labels

The landing adds direct class/relation and navigation-label assertions after view/date setup. Thursday week start and unusual work days expose default assumptions; calendar UTC and best-effort en-US locale are separate inputs. Month and multiweek tasks check only the shown first four grid rows for days off, then await localization before fixed April 2022 week-number labels. These direct checks cover more than merely executing the path, but do not establish live accessibility, all date boundaries or a fresh passing run.

Scope: Calendar browser-view assertion coverage.
