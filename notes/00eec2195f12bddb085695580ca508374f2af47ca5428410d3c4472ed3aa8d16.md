# S/MIME certificate viewing delegates chain URL creation to the platform helper

1931 replaces manual single-certificate base64 URL construction with await getCertViewerUrl(cert), then opens or reuses a mail:3pane tab. Separately inspected Gecko 00aad09a820405643aebd693e60a3333ccf04544 determines usages, picks a best verified chain, falls back to the given certificate and encodes one cert= parameter per element. Its priority begins with TLS server/client before email signer/recipient, so the helper is not an email-only chain selector. That Gecko revision is unpaired context, not the fixed comm endpoint or a certificate verification test. This is static source inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: S/MIME certificate viewer chain.
