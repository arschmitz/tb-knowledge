# Alarm preference menu tests wait for popup states around item activation

The alarm preference helper synthesizes a mouse interaction, waits for the menupopup shown state, activates the requested item and waits for hidden before returning. Its four callers await these operations in sequence. This replaces direct click calls while preserving the existing expected alarm values. Later scroll-position and text-tick fixes are separate phases, and the initial patch is not proof that the intermittent test passed.

Scope: calendar-alarm-preference-menu-completion.
