# Default synthetic messages end with CRLF without rewriting custom bodies

MessageGenerator adds CRLF to its fallback Hello body. The explicit bodyPart and body branches retain caller input, so tests can still supply an intentionally unterminated body. The fake NNTP helper stops appending its own conditional EOL, and the mbox fixture changes its separator calculation to one blank line after messageSize. Review tried a similar extension-helper change and found that its inputs had a different contract; that expansion was not applied. The current NNTP helper has moved to mailnews/test/fakeserver. No message-store or news test ran.

Scope: Synthetic message boundaries.
