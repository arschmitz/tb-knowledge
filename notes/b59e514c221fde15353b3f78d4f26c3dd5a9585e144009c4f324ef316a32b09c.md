# Add-on installation validates the downloaded package before enabling it

AddonInstaller applies its ID/minimum-version checks when those requirements are present and its prompt rejects a mismatching downloaded XPI. After installation it enables the add-on, awaits startupPromise when available, and retains a one-second wait. The comment reports a NOT_AVAILABLE password-verification failure before startup. Preserve the validation/startup boundary; the static inspection does not prove that the remaining delay is sufficient in all cases. This is static source and test-code inspection at the frozen accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator timeline and inline discussion remain pending; a landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: Account Hub add-on package and startup contract.
