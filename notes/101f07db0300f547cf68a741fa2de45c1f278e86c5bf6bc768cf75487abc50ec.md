# Reply selection crosses the child-process boundary as HTML

The 2024 compose helper cleanup removed a type argument that the helper did not use; it did not remove the reply-type gate. At the accepted endpoint, the helper has moved into MailMessageChild. The parent awaits MailMessage:GetSelectionForQuoting and passes a string to the compose service. The child rejects absent or non-range selection, applies the selection preferences, strips citation tags and preserves preformatted quoting. The service still limits selection quoting to reply types. Review callers and process ownership when changing this path; do not recreate parent-side DOM Selection access from the old helper.

Scope: compose.
