# Build listener masks from named constants when bits have holes

The removed unicharPropertyChanged bit remains unused at 0x20; later constants still use 0x40 and 0x80. The current mail-session test builds its combined mask by OR-ing the named interface constants, then checks self-removal during dispatch and subsequent absence. Recommend symbolic-mask construction in this test family instead of assuming consecutive powers of two. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run.
