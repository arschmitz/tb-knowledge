# Show a success notice after initializing the destination Account Hub step

#initUI hides the old subviews, clears the old step notification and sets the new state. Current discovery and sync paths initialize their destination before placing success information in that subview. Adding a notice before #initUI can clear it immediately. For this controller, tie a success message to completed work and the visible destination rather than showing a generic success on every navigation. This does not establish all asynchronous completion ordering outside the inspected paths. These conclusions come from static source inspection. No runtime test or accessibility session was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Account Hub notification transition ownership.
