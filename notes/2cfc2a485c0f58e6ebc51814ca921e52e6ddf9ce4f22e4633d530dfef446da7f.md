# Keep dark-toggle names in their markup and localization namespaces

In the checked header feature, DOM IDs use darkReaderToggle and disableDarkReader; shared classes use toggle-checkbox and toggle-checkbox-sm; Fluent IDs use dark-message-mode-toggle-*. Recommend matching the local camelCase DOM and dashed reusable-class/localization names for related additions. Do not infer a whole-project DOM naming mandate from this feature; legacy header IDs use other forms. These are fixed accepted source and test-code observations.
