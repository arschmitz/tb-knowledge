# Label ordering workarounds as workarounds rather than test naming rules

test_zzz deliberately sorts late so Thunderbird quits and restarts once more while collecting coverage. It works around suspected final-session coverage loss on Windows; it does not verify a new command-line product feature. Preserve the reason in its docstring, and avoid extending zzz naming to ordinary scenarios. Tests should otherwise name the behavior they verify. Historical reports say the coverage workaround helped, but this study did not inspect a fresh coverage artifact or prove the exact loss mechanism.

Scope: Coverage lifecycle and exceptional test naming.
