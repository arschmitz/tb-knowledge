# Historical separator-color consolidation does not fix a permanent opacity rule

The patch removes five Thunderbird separator-color definitions so the imported shared style can supply its value. D252997 describes the intended historical default as currentColor with 25% opacity. Fixed accepted variables.css instead sets --panel-separator-color to --color-surface-border, and panelUI.css still consumes that token. Keep the historical rationale separate from the later producer. Reviewer platform observations do not establish a new computed-color or contrast result.

Scope: Separator color producer and consumer chronology.
