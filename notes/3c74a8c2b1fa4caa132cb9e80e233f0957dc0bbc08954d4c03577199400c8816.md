# Keep notification ranking separate from current-candidate displacement

The manager ranks by lower severity, earlier start_at and lower percent_chance. It still keeps an eligible current notification when its severity is at least as important as the best candidate. Ties in the other ranking fields do not force displacement. A sort helper alone does not define when visible content changes. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: In-app notification candidate stability.
