# MailGlue passes explicit untrusted-process opt-ins with its actor declarations

The comm port marks BrowserProcess and 14 window-actor declarations with safeForUntrustedWebProcess: true, then passes the declaration objects to ActorManagerParent. It does not change a preference or the actor handlers. The accepted source later renames Pdfjs to PdfJs and makes the ContextMenu assignment conditional. These are checked registration fields; the upstream enforcement and handler security contracts were not paired.

Scope: MailGlue actor registration metadata.
