# Read the two address-card naming implementations separately

The native card falls back from generated names to Company, then the primary-email local part. The current AddrBookCard fills extended name parts and tries the email local part before organization in its final fallback. Their tests check missing-name cases separately. This is observed behavior of two implementations, not a proposed universal contact-name policy. This is static source and test-code inspection. Complete Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.
