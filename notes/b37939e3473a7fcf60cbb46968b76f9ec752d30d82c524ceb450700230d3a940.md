# The copy-request destructor reorder was a hypothesis rather than a confirmed crash cure

The assigned patch moves MOZ_COUNT_DTOR after source-object deletion. The author suspects early unlinking, but subsequent reports reproduce the crash and say its rate did not improve. Later double-ClearRequest analysis has explicit same-cause uncertainty. Current listener-before-removal ordering and mPendingRemoval guards are separate evidence linked through existing lifetime lessons. Preserve this chronology instead of teaching the macro reorder as a proven general lifetime fix. No crash test ran.

Scope: Copy-service crash diagnosis.
