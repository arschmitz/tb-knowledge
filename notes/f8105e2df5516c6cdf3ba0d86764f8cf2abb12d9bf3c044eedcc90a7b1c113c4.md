# Local Rust vendoring removes .gitignore and .cargo-ok paths from crates and checksums

cleanup_vendor includes .gitignore and .cargo-ok in its exact-name recursive cleanup list. Its existing per-package loop removes the matched relative paths from the files checksum map and unlinks those paths before writing the checksum JSON. run_tb_rust_vendor calls this local cleanup after vendoring and unwanted-crate checks. This establishes the checked local source flow; it does not establish check-upstream hash behavior or a measured reduction in vendoring differences.

Scope: Thunderbird local Rust vendor cleanup.
