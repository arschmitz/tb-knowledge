# Account Hub's synchronous reset guard does not await its async view resets

The controller assigns currentView.reset() to isClean and immediately tests its truthiness before hiding views and clearing currentView. Both concrete email and address-book reset methods are async and await subview initialization before finishing cleanup and returning true. Their immediate return is a Promise. This guard therefore does not represent completed cleanup or observe the eventual result/rejection. The mismatch is present in the owned and fixed accepted phases; no new regression, reproduced close failure or runtime effect is claimed.

Scope: Account Hub reset completion contract.
