# Build the QR summary from the generation inputs and text nodes

populateSummary runs after codes are generated. It localizes the QR count and account count, fills account names through li.textContent/title and replaceChildren, and selects the passwords label from includePasswords. It does not re-read the form selection when Summary becomes visible or confirm that the mobile device imported anything. Current tests check count arguments, account names/tooltips and both password labels. Use text nodes for account display names rather than interpolating those names into HTML.

Scope: mail/components/preferences.
