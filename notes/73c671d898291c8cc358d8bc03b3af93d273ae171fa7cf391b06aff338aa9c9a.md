# Explain flag-mask merges by the authority of each operand

The local copy comments describe retaining parser flags outside carryOver and source flags inside it. Accepted folder-source copies use New/Read/HasRe; file copies use New/Read/Marked. Keep the explicit merge (newFlags & ~carryOver) | (sourceFlags & carryOver) and document who supplies each set. Do not replace it with an unconditional union or treat the two copy modes as identical. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.
