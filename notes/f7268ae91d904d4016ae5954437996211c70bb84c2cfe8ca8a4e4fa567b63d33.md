Thunderbird registration first calls the Mozilla taskgraph registration function, then assigns generator.verifications to the comm-local VerificationSequence. The local collection contains active checks; it does not disable verification wholesale. The historical module also leaves four checks commented out with TODOs. Keep those exact enabled and disabled scopes separate from reports about upstream cache-validation failures and from later additions or removals.
