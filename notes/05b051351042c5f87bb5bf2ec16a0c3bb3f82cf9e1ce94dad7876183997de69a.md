# New-mail alert resize completion keeps its fallback and later accessibility phases distinct

The resize promise accepts a dimension change and waits one animation frame, or uses a 100 ms positioning fallback. The listener stays installed after initial completion. The original patch moved the alert role onto an empty text container; accepted markup instead keeps the body role and temporarily hides populated text with a later 150 ms timer. Neither a timer settlement nor these attributes proves that an OS or assistive technology reached the intended state.

Scope: Legacy alert resize and accessibility state.
