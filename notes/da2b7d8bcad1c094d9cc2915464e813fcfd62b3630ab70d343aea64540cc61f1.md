# The contact icon suppresses the explicit second click before adding a card

Accepted header-fields stops propagation and returns when event.detail equals 2 before following the edit/add route. This is the component’s double-click guard; it does not establish idempotency for every repeated or synthetic click count. The browser test synthesizes clickCount 2 and a normal click, then checks duplicate behavior and lookup after deletion without reproducing native event ordering. This is static source and test-code inspection. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: mail/base/content.
