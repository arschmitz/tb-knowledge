# Address-book search registers density and font updates after configuring its tree

The search dialog configures its AutoTreeView and listeners before registering the window with density/font services. Registration immediately applies current settings; density dispatches uidensitychange and the tree can reset its row height. Font registration also applies settings and emits a change. Both services clean up on unload. This explains the local initialization order; unchanged search assertions do not directly test that order. No dialog or accessibility execution.

Scope: addressbook-tree-startup.
