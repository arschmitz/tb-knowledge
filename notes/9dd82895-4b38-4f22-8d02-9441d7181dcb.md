# Keep the old Calendar summary dialog sizing workaround scoped

After the toolkit flexbox change, the old Calendar summary dialog could open too short. This May 2024 workaround sets a resize class during load and removes it after Fluent finishes, instead of relying on sizeToContent. Review treated it as temporary because the old dialogs were planned for replacement.

Scope: Calendar summary dialog.
