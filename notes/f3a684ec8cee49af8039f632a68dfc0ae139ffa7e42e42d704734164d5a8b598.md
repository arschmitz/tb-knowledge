# The Calendar system-principal workaround was withdrawn while ICS name decoding remained

Fresh full two-file principal/name change read. The landing substitutes system principal in new calendar channels and decodes the ICS path before choosing a default name. Later available Bug/review discussion explicitly agrees to back out most of the principal change after same-domain/different-auth regressions, withdraws ESR intent and continues elsewhere. Original acceptance/Nextcloud smoke reports are not current policy. Fixed prepHttpChannel again creates a content principal with originAttributes and per-user container separation, with optional fresh-auth context and existing-channel reuse. Fixed ICS handleCalendar retains decodeURI naming. This independently distinguishes the reverted mechanism from the retained remainder. Related reverting source/review and Gecko ORB internals were not independently opened. No Calendar server, auth-isolation, URL or network test ran; no global principal rule is inferred.

Scope: Calendar channel principal and auth separation.
