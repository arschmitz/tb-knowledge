# Keep MIME decoder callback signatures typed through initialization

931 changes MimeConverterOutputCallback and decoder initializers to accept MimeClosure, removing function-pointer cast workarounds in the changed consumers. The accepted public decoder helper declarations keep the typed callback/value pair. Preserve that signature through typedefs, initializer functions and callers instead of casting an incompatible callback to silence the compiler. The series changed no tests in these files and no C++ build was run.

Scope: MIME decoder callback signature syntax.
