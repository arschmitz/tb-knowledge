# Panorama disables the legacy FolderCache without removing the legacy path

The assigned patch bypasses legacy FolderCache work when the Panorama startup preference is enabled and gives several direct entrypoints explicit NOT_IMPLEMENTED results. GetFolderCache also clears its out pointer. Non-Panorama behavior remains. The full Bug and review explain why wholesale removal was rejected: the legacy per-folder database may still need the cache on profiles with many folders. Repeated guards intentionally expose the boundary; they are not evidence of measured overhead or a general preference-check style rule. An assertion alone is not a release-mode guard. Static source inspection only.

Scope: Panorama and legacy folder-cache boundary.
