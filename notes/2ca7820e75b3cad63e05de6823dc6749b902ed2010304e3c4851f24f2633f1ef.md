# Icon payload renames can invert the condition used by the consumer

Entry 797 replaces canUseForTab with isRichIcon and inverts the tab-icon condition. Current LinkHandlerParent receives the full data object and destructures iconURL, images and isRichIcon. Only a non-rich icon reaches setTabFavIcon after URL and principal checks. Current image conversion awaits work and then rechecks that the browser still belongs to a tab. Manual actor contract proposal: read the producer meaning and the consumer condition together when renaming a boolean; a change from permission wording to category wording is not a simple textual rename. Keep the post-await ownership check. The Gecko child producer was not inspected in this comm-only source pass, so full cross-process payload compatibility remains a limit.

Scope: content-tab-icon-contract.
