# Validate extension URLs before constructing their triggering principal

Accepted ExtensionUtilities explicitly assigns context.checkLoadURL to callers. tabs.create/update and windows.create perform that check before calling getTriggeringPrincipalForTabCreate. The helper constructs an about: target principal or clones the extension context with userContextId and privateBrowsingId zero for other schemes. A permission-bearing principal is not itself proof that an extension may open an arbitrary URL. This is source and test inspection only. Full Bugzilla and historical review discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran.

Scope: mail/components/extensions.
