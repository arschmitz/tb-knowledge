# Flatpak runtime permissions and dictionary paths are packaging contracts

The fixed accepted repack metadata declares Wayland and fallback-x11 and DICPATH=/usr/share/hunspell. These values configure the packaged runtime; preserve their scope when moving launch or packaging steps. Entry532 restores DICPATH after the reported rename migration. Entry534 removes the earlier metadata TMPDIR override. Its complete human discussion now gives an explicit backout decision: a commenter disputes literal tilde and launcher override behavior, while the reporter later cannot reproduce and says removing the metadata line makes no difference. Those reports do not prove shell expansion, a portal cause or the effective runtime environment. The current metadata omits TMPDIR; the historical launch_script.sh path is absent at the fixed revision. Read the actual launcher/configuration used by a new packaging change before assigning a temporary-file path. No installed Flatpak, backend, dictionary lookup or attachment launch was exercised.

Scope: packaging.
