# The getContexts test compares document IDs with loaded-page reports

Loaded pages supply their documentId to the browser task, which includes that ID in expected contexts. New assertions distinguish a navigated document from the prior tab document and compare background top/subframe IDs with their corresponding reports. These are direct identity-correlation assertions. They do not establish targeted messaging, port routing or independent correctness of the inherited identity provider. No browser test ran.

Scope: MailExtension document identity test scope.
