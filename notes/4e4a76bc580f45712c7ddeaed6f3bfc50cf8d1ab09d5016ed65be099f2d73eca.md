# Commands console and shared Thunderbird memory system

USearch 2.26.4 graph segments are immutable, at most 4,096 vectors, partitioned by repository/model/dimension; SQLite original vectors remain authoritative and candidates are rescored with exact arithmetic. Fall back to exact search for explicit filters, small/stale/incomplete/damaged indexes, unsupported vectors, read-only snapshots, unavailable native bindings, and near ties. Index updates use source-generation journaling, checksum/shape validation, atomic publication, and scheduled batches of at most 16 rows. [Task 8]
