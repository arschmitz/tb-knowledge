# Wait for asynchronous about3Pane initialization as well as document load

waitForMailTabReady waits for an about:3pane document load and then hasDOMContentLoaded.promise. The promise resolves after the async DOMContentLoaded handler initializes panes and restores state. mailTabs.create awaits this helper before updates. Preserve both readiness boundaries; document.readyState alone does not establish that async initialization work has finished. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run.

Scope: mail/components/extensions.
