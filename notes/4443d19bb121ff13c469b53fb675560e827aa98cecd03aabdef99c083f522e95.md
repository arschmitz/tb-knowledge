# mimeSignatureData identifies the extracted detached MIME part

The landing renames sigData to mimeSignatureData across initialization, extraction and verification. Current parser decodes base64 or quoted-printable signature-part data, extracts the armored text and passes that field separately from signedData to verifyDetached. Scoped naming recommendation: keep MIME signature bytes distinct from signed message bytes and status/result data. The rename is not proof of a crypto-format change or a global prefix rule. This is static source and test-code inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending; source landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: OpenPGP data naming.
