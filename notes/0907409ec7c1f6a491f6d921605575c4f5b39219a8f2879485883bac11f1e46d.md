# Removing pageLoading leaves tab-specific pageLoaded readiness signals intact

The cleanup deletes the unused pageLoading writes, while pageLoaded remains the consumed state. Content tabs update it for network load transitions, the address book waits for about-addressbook-ready, and preferences waits for paneSelected. These signals have different meanings; removing a redundant field does not unify readiness or wait for later asynchronous pane selection.

Scope: tab-readiness-state.
