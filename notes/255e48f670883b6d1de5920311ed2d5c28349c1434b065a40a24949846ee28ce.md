# The infobar icon reset belongs to the Thunderbird chrome override and its current selector phase

The Thunderbird jar overrides the global infobar resource with its shared skin stylesheet. This landing resets the icon-container border, radius, background and padding, and removes an explicit button-part text color. Accepted source later adds a margin reset and narrows the button selector to the close control. Keep those phases separate and assess actual cascade/receiver markup before generalizing the reset. No resolved color, contrast, geometry or live accessibility result was measured.

Scope: Thunderbird infobar icon styling.
