### makeFriendlyDateAgo (39–66)

Creates current time and local midnight. Time at/after midnight, including future values, gets short time. Older values less than24hours before midnight get minus1day relative label; less than6*24hours get weekday; older same-year dates get month/day; other years get short date. Fixed millisecond thresholds are not calendar-day subtraction, so daylight-saving edges can differ from comments.
