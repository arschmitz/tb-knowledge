# Offline replay calls the IMAP lite-select service directly with its continuation listener

The folder-level liteSelect IDL/wrapper is removed. Non-pseudo offline replay with pending IMAP operations instead resets current UID validity and calls LiteSelectFolder on the IMAP service with itself as URL listener. The service creates a liteselect operation; OnStopRunningUrl remains the continuation. The checked no-header-sync contract and early return are static source evidence, not a remote completion or failure-path-equivalence result.

Scope: IMAP offline playback selection.
