# AdjustRowCount documents its selection constraint before tree notifications

AdjustRowCount checks that there is no selection before notifying its trees of a nonzero row-count change. The inspected search-view sort caller saves and clears selection before this call and restores it afterward. The historical save-to-safe change corrects only the assertion text; it does not add a guard, alter the predicate or prove runtime safety.

Scope: DB view row-count diagnostic and caller order.
