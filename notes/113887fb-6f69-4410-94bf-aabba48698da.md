# CalDAV response chunks are raw bytes

CalDAV response chunks are raw bytes. Decode once after assembling the whole body with the server's declared charset; decoding each chunk as UTF-8 fails for UTF-16 and split characters. Cross-origin DAV redirects with an `Authorization` header abort instead of carrying credentials to another server. [charset commit](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-desktop/commit/f65f05177086), [redirect commit](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-desktop/commit/cf52c835e3d5).
