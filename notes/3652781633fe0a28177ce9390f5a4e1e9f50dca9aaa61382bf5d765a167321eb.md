# Thundermail bridge scope and message checks changed after the original import

The original manifest injects token-bridge.js at document_start on <all_urls>. The original page-message listener leaves its origin and source guards commented out, then forwards token/user, sign-in/out and pending-token requests. The inspected owned background receiver handles account/token storage and replies with a staged token to the sender tab without a general sender-origin guard. Accepted source later limits injection to the two Send origins and actively checks e.source and e.origin. The accepted OIDC_USER case also refuses to replace an existing session; the pending-token case still replies by sender tab ID. These are exact static source phases. They do not establish an exploit, permission bypass, token disclosure or runtime safety result.

Scope: Thundermail content-script to privileged-background message bridge.
