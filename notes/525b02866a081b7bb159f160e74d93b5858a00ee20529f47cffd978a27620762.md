# Current compose startup selects an initial focus target instead of always focusing the body

The removed SetFocusOnStartup unconditionally called gContentWindow.focus. Current AdjustFocus picks the NNTP newsgroup or To container, focuses its input if it has no pills, otherwise an empty subject, and finally the body. The current body helper advances focus into messageArea because its comment says window.content.focus does not blur the old focused element. Keep the actual priority and focus operation when reviewing startup. That reported focus limitation was not reproduced or attributed to the deletion. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: compose initial focus.
