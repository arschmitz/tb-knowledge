# Let a form-less button fall through to its enclosing link

928 requires a form action before taking the input/button branch, so a button without a form can reach the enclosing-anchor search. That legacy contentAreaClick helper is absent in the accepted revision; the actor now delegates href extraction to BrowserUtils. Keep the historical repair and current delegation separate. The malformed nested-control MIME fixture is a regression input, not an HTML style example or evidence of accessible nested controls.

Scope: Message content link extraction.
