# The themed messenger body declaration keeps its exact color-scheme token and scope

This landing adds the literal color-scheme: light-dark under :root[lwtheme] body, and accepted source keeps it. Preserve that spelling and selector. It is distinct from the light-dark() function and a space-separated light dark list. Source identity does not establish parser treatment, computed scheme or a rendered theme cure; those engine/runtime checks remain a gap.

Scope: Messenger body theme declaration.
