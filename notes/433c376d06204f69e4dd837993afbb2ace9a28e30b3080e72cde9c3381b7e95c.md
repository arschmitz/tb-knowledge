# Invitation overlay failure does not erase the parsed iTIP item

CalMimeConverter catches legacy invitation overlay creation or serialization, leaves the overlay empty, and still assigns mailChannel.imipItem. Parsing and itipItem.init are outside that catch. This preserves the parsed item for later invitation handling when only rendering fails. The related missing-startDate comparator guard is separate. Its changed fixture was explicitly reported not to reproduce that failure, and the inspected cancellation test checks deletion and no response rather than the comparator or error log.

Scope: Calendar MIME rendering.
