# Treat an empty NNTP transfer as a failed message, even when completion says NS_OK

NntpChannel has two failure signals. A non-NS_OK completion dooms the cache. A completion with no payload also dooms it and sets URI.errorCode to NS_ERROR_FAILURE to prevent marking the message read. The listener completion status is a separate value. When changing this path, test repeated reads after a failed fetch, offline/online transitions and more than one cached connection. The patch has no new automated test; recovery is reported in the bug/review. The causal reason for the one accepted tee onStartRequest catch remained partly unexplained.

Scope: mailnews/news.
