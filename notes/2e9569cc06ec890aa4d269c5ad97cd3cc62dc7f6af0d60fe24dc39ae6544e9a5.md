# Preserve the parser minimum-chunk contract across short reads

The mbox parser treats input smaller than MinChunk as EOF. The accepted PumpData repeatedly reads until MinChunk or a real zero-byte read, then feeds the parser. A short underlying read is not itself EOF. Any caller or buffering refactor must preserve that contract. Only the listed current loop and historical parser change were read, not a new short-read runtime experiment.

Scope: Mbox stream chunk contracts.
