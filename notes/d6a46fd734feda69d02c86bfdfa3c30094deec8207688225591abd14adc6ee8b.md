# Distinguish account creation success from optional sync discovery failure

After the account exists, #fetchSyncAccounts chooses either the added-success step or the sync-selection step. Discovery failure still moves to the added-success step; a user skip receives success while another error receives an info notice about missing sync accounts. Do not label that later address-book or calendar discovery failure as failed email account creation. The inspected source is not proof that every provider discovery succeeds. These conclusions come from static source inspection. No runtime test or accessibility session was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Account Hub account and sync outcome scope.
