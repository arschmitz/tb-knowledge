# Exclude pending totals before counting completed compose copies

The compose copy follow-up subtracts numPendingTotalMessages from getTotalMessages(false) before waiting for one saved message. For a non-server folder, GetTotalMessages includes pending totals, so a positive total alone can be premature. The later accepted helper also subtracts the initial nonpending count, waits for an increase and separately waits until header enumeration exposes the copy before checking its subject and fake-server message. Preserve these separate readiness conditions when changing the test. The one-line follow-up does not prove remote completion or a measured failure-rate improvement, and no test ran.

Scope: Compose copy-test synchronization.
