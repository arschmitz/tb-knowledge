# The password-manager policy test yields on its returned preferences window

The password-manager test awaits openPreferencesTab and receives the preferences Window. Its next task is scheduled with prefWin.setTimeout, and its assertion reads showPasswords.disabled from prefWin.document. The policy setup and final tab closure remain in place. This scopes the timer receiver and direct assertion; it does not show that every preferences tab path settles or that one timer task proves general UI readiness. The isolated debug failure and its cure were not reproduced.

Scope: Enterprise policy browser tests.
