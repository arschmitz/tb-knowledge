# Expire notification rate limits from the oldest retained timestamp

The manager retains six selection timestamps. When its rolling window is full, it waits until the oldest timestamp plus the time unit, rather than waiting one new full day from the latest attempt. The updater uses the same oldest-retained principle with its separate 24-update history. Keep the two counts and time windows distinct in changes and tests. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: In-app notification rolling limits.
