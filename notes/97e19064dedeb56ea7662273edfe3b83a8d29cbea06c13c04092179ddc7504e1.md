# Carry Bcc separately without inserting it into transmitted MIME

EWS To/Cc recipients already exist in MIME content; Bcc is passed separately because its visible header is excluded. Current EWS CreateItem sends explicit bcc_recipients and DSN while SMTP appends Bcc to its envelope recipient list. Keep delivery recipients separate from visible message headers. Shared Graph Bcc/DSN regression knowledge52ebd8... was checked against current test_graph_bcc_dsn. That test does not by itself prove EWS Bcc wire output.

Scope: rust/ews_xpcom/src/client.
