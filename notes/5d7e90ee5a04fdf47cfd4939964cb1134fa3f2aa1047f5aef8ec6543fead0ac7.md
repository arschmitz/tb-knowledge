# The UID-validity warning leaves the invalidation branch in place

The patch replaces a kUidUnknown-conditioned assertion with a warning inside the UID-validity mismatch branch. It leaves the database backup/recreation, validity/reset markers and fresh-fetch work in place. The fixed accepted source moves that work into HandleUidInvalidation and keeps the warning at entry. This is a diagnostic/control-flow observation, not proof that invalidation, offline-copy intermittents or debug failures are cured.

Scope: IMAP UID invalidation diagnostics.
