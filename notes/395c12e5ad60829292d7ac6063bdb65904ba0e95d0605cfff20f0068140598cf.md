# Offline read invalidation follows incoming stream status and its preference

OfflineMessageReadListener first forwards stop to its destination. It discards the offline copy only when the incoming stream status failed and mail.discard_offline_msg_on_failure is true, then clears its channel reference and returns the destination result. A destination error alone is not that discard condition. Discard permits a later download; it is not an immediate retry or proof the next read will succeed. No stream or recovery test ran.

Scope: OfflineMessageReadListener completion and retry boundary.
