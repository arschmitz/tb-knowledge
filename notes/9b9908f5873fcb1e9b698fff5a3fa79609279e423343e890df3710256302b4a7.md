# Storage queries yield at processing checkpoints without bounding each recurrence expansion

Event and todo stream sources check elapsed Date.now time after nonrecurring row enqueue and after eligible recurring-parent expansion. When elapsed time is greater than 15 ms, they await a timeout with no explicit delay argument. The timer check comes after synchronous getOccurrences, and max-count exits can skip it, so it does not guarantee a frame rate or cap every processing pause. The database wrapper awaits row callbacks and result-set promises before completion, and the bounded stream awaits source.start. Preserve those completion boundaries when changing the checkpoints.

Scope: Calendar storage stream scheduling.
