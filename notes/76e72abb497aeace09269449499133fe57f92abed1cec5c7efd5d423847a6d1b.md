# The original Thundermail enabled-pref assertion checks the form rather than the sign-in control

The new test's message says the sign-in button should be visible, but it passes newForm to BrowserTestUtils.isVisible. A visible form does not directly assert visibility of its Thundermail header or button. The later accepted test instead selects the new header, waits for it and asserts that control's visibility and placement. Preserve this later correction without treating the original test as sign-in, focus or OAuth coverage. Its loaded historical fixture also retains a hidden Thundermail header and lacks the production middle-content forwarding slots. Keep fixture markup and production markup separate when judging the assertion.

Scope: Account Hub test target and fixture boundary.
