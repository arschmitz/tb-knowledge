# Deleted-items shutdown removes its observer before invoking the manager

The local xpcom-shutdown observer removes this observer from Services.obs before invoking self.shutdown on the captured deleted-items manager. The two identities remain distinct, and the one-shot registration is made only after initDB opens the database and handles its schema. shutdown starts asyncClose and clears mDB; the callback does not await database closure. This records the scoped replacement for the removed generic helper, not a global observer or closure style rule. No shutdown or database result was exercised.

Scope: Calendar deleted-items shutdown lifecycle.
