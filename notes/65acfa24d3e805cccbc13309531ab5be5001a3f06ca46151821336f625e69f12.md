# Whitespace conversion preserves wrap points outside HTML tags

The accepted flowed-text helper turns tab expansion and selected spaces into &nbsp;, while preserving the final ordinary space when wrapping is allowed. Line_convert_whitespace tracks tag/quote state and leaves tag contents unchanged. This explains why the loop counters and convert_all_whitespace flag exist.
