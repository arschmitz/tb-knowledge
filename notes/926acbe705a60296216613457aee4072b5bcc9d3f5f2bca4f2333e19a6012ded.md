# Current encrypted remote-content policy has an integrity condition

The 2024 content-policy consumer rejects remote content when the shared encrypted service reports the requesting message encrypted. At the fixed accepted revision, that consumer instead asks IsEncryptedWithoutIntegrity; the OpenPGP producer grants integrity protection only for an intact top-level message rather than a subpart or partially encrypted view. Do not describe the current policy as a blanket ban on all encrypted remote content. These consumer/producer branches were inspected as a limited current comparison; the later policy change is not fully studied, and no security or remote-load behavior was tested. Full bug and review discussion remains pending.

Scope: Encrypted remote-content policy.
