# Remove the scheduler’s idle observer on application quit

NotificationScheduler registers with the idle service using threshold30 and removes that observer on quit-application. This breaks the selected registration relationship described by the Bug; it does not prove all scheduler references or failed-startup cases are cleaned. No leak or shutdown test ran.

Scope: Notification idle-service lifetime.
