# Loaded long titles have a validation exception

For D334596 at revision e70986137560511be0adbbbcdab0539b30ef1191, the AI review reports that an unchanged loaded Calendar event title over 180 characters remains valid because assigning input.value during loading does not set validity.tooLong. This explains why the dialog uses native validity rather than string length. A focused experiment reportedly kept a loaded 200-character title valid after focus left and another field changed; editing the title made it invalid. Preserve this loaded-title distinction when reviewing the validation check. Suggested coverage checks dialog.canSave before and after changing another field, since preserved text alone does not prove Save eligibility. Both checks reportedly passed, and a temporary Backspace action made them fail. This is a scoped review and experiment claim, not independently confirmed behavior for other controls or revisions. The comments remain pending, and no current runtime defect was reported.

Scope: Calendar event title validation.
