# Extension policy cleanup separates optional-permission removal from state refresh

The inspected ExtensionSettings callback skips system, built-in and non-profile add-ons, computes the intersection of granted optional permissions and blocked_permissions, awaits removal only when that intersection is nonempty, and then requests an add-on disabled-state refresh. Required-permission effects are delegated to platform helpers and are not proven here. The original landing and later accepted update_url/reporting additions are separate phases; no permission mutation or add-on-manager runtime was executed.

Scope: Enterprise policies.
