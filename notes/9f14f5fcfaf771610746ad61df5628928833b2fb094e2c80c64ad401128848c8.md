# Avoid declaring a synchronous component initializer async without a contract reason

Current connectedCallback is marked async but its full body has no await and performs shadow/template setup synchronously. Proposed local syntax check: remove an unnecessary async marker when no promise contract or asynchronous operation is needed, or document the reason for keeping it. This is a scoped recommendation, not a claim that the marker broke the build or a new approved project-wide rule. This is static source and test-code inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending; source landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: calendar callback syntax.
