# Remove the build export together with an obsolete public header

Entry 1810 deletes nsMsgHeaderMasks.h and nsMsgGroupnameFlags.h and removes the HeaderMasks EXPORTS entry. The deleted masks header already marked itself DO NOT USE ANYMORE. Preserve the build-list part when removing this kind of retired header; its old guard style and bit masks are historical evidence rather than recommended current syntax. This study read the deletion, not a successful build or a repository-wide absence proof. Static source and test inspection only. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran.
