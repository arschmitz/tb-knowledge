# The attendees-day skip checks UTC minute 59 at task registration

Only the final day-range task is skipped when its registration-time JavaScript clock says UTC hour 23 and minute 59. The manifest forces Calendar timezone UTC, while fromToday caches the Calendar date at its first call and reuses that midnight for offsets. This mitigation does not continuously watch the clock or stop an earlier registered task from crossing midnight. It is a source condition, not a fresh intermittent or timezone result.

Scope: Calendar browser-test day boundary.
