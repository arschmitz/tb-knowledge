# Keep virtual-folder name validation distinct from local-store hashing

virtualFolderProperties still rejects a leading dot, trailing dot/tilde/space or semicolon/hash through a regex and prevents dialog acceptance. Its TODO questions whether native hashing could permit some names. This comment is an open design question, not authorization to remove the UI validation or a proof that all names are invalid on every store. Keep the virtual-folder rule scoped when reviewing name-handling changes. These are fixed accepted source and test-code observations. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint, live accessibility or crypto/TLS validation ran.
