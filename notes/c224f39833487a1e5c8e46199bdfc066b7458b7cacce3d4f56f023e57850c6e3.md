# MIME and MAPI file boundaries clear their local macros and use typed headers

The owned patch appends 29 MIME_SUPERCLASS and two MAPI_STARTUP_ARG undef directives. Several MIME files replace hand-written base-typed extern declarations with actual class headers. Related and Alternative headers declare their own concrete class types. Accepted multipart source retains those includes and the file-end undef; another changed historical file, mimeebod.cpp, no longer exists there. The finding is limited to these compilation boundaries and does not require clearing unrelated macros or prove a successful build.

Scope: MailNews native include and macro boundaries.
