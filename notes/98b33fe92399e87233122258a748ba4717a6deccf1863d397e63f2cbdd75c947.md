# Treat unit-only localization as a changed text contract

Proposal for Calendar duration strings: derive a unit-only menu label from an existing translation only when the translated meaning and plural forms remain valid. The 561 review rejects literal count removal and a one/other-only migration: locales can use additional categories or spell out one. It also identifies the changed meaning as a reason for new strings. The initial count-free event-duration-unit change was backed out; later reland separates menu/DOM forms from readable count-bearing phrases. Check each value, .label, .value and aria-label consumer and its loaded Fluent resources. Do not reuse accessibility text as visible text merely because the words look similar. Minimum test coverage: the final counted reminder phrase and the unit-only control in the relevant plural cases. Later reland discussion is context, not full future source coverage; exact failing jobs remain unread.

Scope: calendar localization.
