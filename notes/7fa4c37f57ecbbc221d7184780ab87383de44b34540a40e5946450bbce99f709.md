# Biff scheduling compares full deadlines and deliberately adds one millisecond

SetupNextBiff compares full microsecond deadlines before a server-key tie-break, instead of narrowing their difference to int. Its timer delay is floor(microseconds/1000)+1, so even zero and exact-millisecond values gain one millisecond; this is not exact ceiling division. PerformBiff now treats equality with currentTime as due. The separate overdue branch still chooses30seconds before conversion. These are checked source changes, not a reproduced polling or sleep/resume result.

Scope: Mail polling scheduler.
