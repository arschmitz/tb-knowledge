# The Calendar monitor stores nsISound while its alarm path calls the sound helper

The constructor stores getService(Ci.nsISound) as mSound, but the inspected onAlarm sound branch calls NotificationSounds.playCustomSound after its own preference and rate checks. The stored field appears only in the constructor and prototype declaration in this module. The nearby all-sounds comment does not identify the actual playback receiver. No native sound or alarm test ran.

Scope: Calendar alarm sound receiver.
