# A visible Account Hub welcome fixture does not establish localized welcome text

The original bare-header fixture has an empty welcome span with no data-l10n-id. Its first-run test checks isVisible for that node, without checking its translated text. Production supplies account-hub-welcome-text, and accepted fixture source later adds that ID. Keep a visibility assertion separate from a content/localization assertion and from actual screen-reader output.

Scope: account-hub-tests.
