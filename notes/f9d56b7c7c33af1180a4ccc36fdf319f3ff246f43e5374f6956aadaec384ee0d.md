# Catch an observed boundary failure and log the operation with its exception

This is a proposed manual review rule for NNTP-style async boundaries. Keep a catch tied to a demonstrated failure and preserve diagnostic context. D208839 removed catches at sites that did not fail and removed the unproved timeout change. The accepted server tee catch logs onStartRequest FAILED together with e. Do not generalize this historical decision into permission to swallow every request error; the reviewer explicitly questioned cancellation and the author could not fully explain why this catch was necessary.

Scope: mailnews/news.
