# Add-on detail links changed from content-tab navigation to external dispatch

The original landing uses the top chrome window's openTrustedLinkIn to open ordinary HTTP(S) anchors and recommendation-card background clicks in tabs. Its test registers a next-tab watcher before clicking and asserts the loaded URL. Fixed accepted ordinary anchors instead call LinkHelper.openLinkExternally with addToHistory:false, and the test checks a mocked external load plus the retained current about:addons tab. The recommendation-card background handler still opens a tab. Keep those source phases and click targets separate; the mock does not prove a real external browser launched.

Scope: about:addons click receivers and browser tests.
