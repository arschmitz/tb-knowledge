# Recognize the newer Teams meet URL form

Microsoft Teams links can use /meet/ as well as the older /l/meetup-join/ path. The join-link parser needs a pattern and test for the new form or the Calendar dialog will miss a valid join action.

Scope: calendar-join-links.
