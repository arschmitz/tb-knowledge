# Document the QR authorization Promise and cancellation outcome

The accepted QR requestAuthorizationToIncludePasswords method is async but its comment still declares a boolean return. The caller awaits it and aborts export on false; the wizard initializer documents Promise<void>. Scoped documentation recommendation: describe Promise<boolean> and what refusal does for this authorization helper. The comment is not proof of a synchronous return or a project-wide JSDoc mandate. This is source and test inspection only. Full Bugzilla and historical review discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran.
