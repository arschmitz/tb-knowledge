# The 2024 XUL dropdown markup is historical beside today’s account-hub-select

Entry 1289 gives native XUL menupopups position="after_start" and styles selected menuitems. The fixed accepted incoming form now uses account-hub-select with option children and l10n-label-id, so do not copy the old XUL markup as the current form pattern. The template keeps lowerCamelCase control IDs such as incomingProtocol and hyphenated component tags and Fluent IDs. These are scoped naming observations for this family, not a rule to rename legacy dotted IDs elsewhere. No popup, keyboard, focus or accessibility behavior was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Account Hub dropdown markup.
