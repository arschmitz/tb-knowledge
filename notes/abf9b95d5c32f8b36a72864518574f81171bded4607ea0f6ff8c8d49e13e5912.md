# Opaque CMS admission can remain provisional until decoding identifies the inner layer

When smime-type is absent, the classifier can provisionally route a candidate into the opaque CMS decoder. EOF then checks actual signed/encrypted state against the parent/depth combination before outputting decoded content. Ignoring an outer signature also resets an already reported signature for the selected message. Keep admission, rendering and signature identity separate; accepted content_info and parent-routing refinements are later phases, and the multipart-signature route has its own checks.

Scope: smime-cms-layering.
