# Encrypted deletion tests assert rejection without proving stored-byte preservation

The new S/MIME and OpenPGP/MIME tasks first list decrypted attachments and check their partName values, then await assertRejects for deletion with the encrypted-operation error. These are direct rejection checks on two prepared fixtures. They do not compare the stored raw message after the rejected operation or prove every encryption/key-availability case. Each new task also begins with browser.test.assertEq(1, messages.length) without a message argument, while its later attachment and rejection assertions have expected-behavior messages. When editing those initial checks, add a message such as "The fixture folder should contain one message." Keep the tested layer, partName and outcome explicit.

Scope: mail/extensions/messages/tests.
