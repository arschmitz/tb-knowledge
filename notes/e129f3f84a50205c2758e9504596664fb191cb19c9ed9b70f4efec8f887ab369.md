# OpenPGP compose inlined two header forwarding helpers at their call sites

D254173 replaces setAdditionalHeader/unsetAdditionalHeader with direct gMsgCompose.compFields.setHeader/deleteHeader calls and removes the one-line wrappers. The author says the calls avoid jumping back and forth; edicharry explicitly agrees that this is easier to read. The wrappers added no checks or policy and discarded no result, so this is a scoped readability example rather than a general instruction to inline helpers. Current draft-status writing and pre-send deletion retain the direct calls. The original Autocrypt insertion is absent from the later current onSendOpenPGP method. The draft header spelling changes to lowercase, while both original and current structured-header APIs lowercase keys, preserving that stored key. No draft/send test or MIME round trip ran.

Scope: OpenPGP compose header preparation.
