# Keep Exchange completion notification separate from success status

The studied EWS landing records destination headers after their database commits and emits NotifyMsgsMoveCopyCompleted when a source folder exists. Its completion route does not require a successful status before that notification. The selected current Exchange completion method keeps that distinction; the notification interface carries source/destination headers and the move flag, with no success-status parameter. Treat this as a completion/payload contract, not proof that every requested message copied successfully. Creation, deletion and combined rename/move notifications have separate producer points. No copy or listener ran, and no complete listener dependency graph was studied.

Scope: EWS/Exchange message and folder notification producers.
