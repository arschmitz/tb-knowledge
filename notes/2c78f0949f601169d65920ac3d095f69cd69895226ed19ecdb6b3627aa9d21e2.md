# Proposed regression coverage for locked account deletion after the control move

Entry683 removes the delete-control assertion from browser_actions while retaining a scenario that locks mail.disable_button.delete_account. The moved am-main Delete button is gated by protocolInfo.canDelete; the inspected command also checks that capability before opening confirmation. These source checks do not establish that every administrator policy still disables deletion. Proposed focused test: lock the deletion preference, select a deletable account, verify the child-page Delete control stays disabled and cannot start removal, then verify normal behavior after cleanup. Keep its setup and assertions distinct from default-account and account-addition checks. No deletion or policy bypass was reproduced, and the historical author Try-pass report does not fill this specific assertion gap.

Scope: account-settings-policy-tests.
