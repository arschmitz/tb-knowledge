# OpenPGP alias-load warnings resolve the public load call after caught failures

With a configured alias_rules_file, load catches every _loadFromFile error and warns through the lazy openpgp logger. It resolves without a success flag instead of forwarding that caught rejection to compose startup. The internal loader clears maps before reading; this is not prior-state preservation or atomic reload, and a later rule exception can leave entries already inserted. An empty preference clears aliases without warning. Keep the public caught-error completion distinct from successful alias loading. Source inspection only; no file, compose or encryption test ran.

Scope: OpenPGP alias loader diagnostics and async completion.
