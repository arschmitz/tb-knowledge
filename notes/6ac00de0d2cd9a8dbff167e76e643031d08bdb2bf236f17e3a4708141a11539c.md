# Keep Linux repack archive format and decompressor dependencies together

In the Flatpak and Snap repack scripts, the downloaded files are .tar.xz and extraction explicitly uses tar J. The Snap image installs xz-utils. The earlier suffix migration at entry 1369 needed this later extraction repair at 1668. Recommend changing the download path, tar compression option and container dependencies together in this owned packaging scope. This is not a successful repack or archive download test.
