# Removing the Rust opt-out makes the comm Cargo override unconditional

Fresh full configure change removes --disable-thunderbird-rust and the conditional resolver. The landing makes MOZ_THUNDERBIRD_RUST, MOZ_OVERRIDE_GKRUST and the comm Cargo override unconditional; it chooses removal of an obsolete opt-out after the reported linker failure rather than repairing a Rust-free build. Fixed source retains the unconditional Cargo workspace override but no longer declares the old MOZ_THUNDERBIRD_RUST name in this selected block. The mail/moz.build consumer includes ../rust when MOZ_OVERRIDE_GKRUST is true. This does not prove all platforms build or diagnose the attached linker log; the compressed log and Gecko configure consumer were not inspected. No configure/build ran.

Scope: Thunderbird Rust build configuration.
