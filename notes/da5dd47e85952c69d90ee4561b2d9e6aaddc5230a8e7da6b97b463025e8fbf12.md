# BondOpenPGP initialization ownership does not itself prove completion for callers

The removal centralizes EnigmailCore.init under BondOpenPGP.init. Bond sets _alreadyTriedInit before its awaits, so another call returns without joining the first initialization. The selected main and compose startup callers do not await it. Core itself sets _initialized after database setup and registration. These sources establish ownership and ordering, but do not prove every consumer sees completed startup. No runtime race or OpenPGP initialization was tested.

Scope: OpenPGP initialization completion scope.
