# The removed unichar preference helper is distinct from the localized helper

Entry 1216 removes NS_GetUnicharPreferenceWithDefault, whose parent-revision exact-symbol search finds only its declaration and implementation. The accepted header still declares NS_GetLocalizedUnicharPreferenceWithDefault. Preserve that name distinction when diagnosing preference code; the cleanup does not show that localized preference defaults were removed. This is a source-backed namespace distinction, not a complete study of all preference consumers. No compose or preference test was run. Full bug and review discussion remains pending.

Scope: Mail preference helper cleanup.
