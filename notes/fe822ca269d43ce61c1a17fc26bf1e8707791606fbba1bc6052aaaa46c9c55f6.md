# Release the encoded term after EncodeImapTerm failure

Entry750 adds delete[] termEncoding before EncodeImap breaks when EncodeImapTerm fails or returns no term. The tested result belongs to EncodeImapTerm, not AddSearchTerm. On success the temporary is deleted after AddSearchTerm consumes it; the accumulated expression has separate cleanup. EncodeImapTerm sets its output to nullptr only after validating term and the output pointer. Keep those preconditions when reasoning about early returns. This corrects the older lesson's failure attribution. No failure injection or memory checker was run.

Scope: IMAP search expression ownership.
