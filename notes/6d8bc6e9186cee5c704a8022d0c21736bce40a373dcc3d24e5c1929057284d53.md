# Commit parsed envelope fields together after validation

840 ParseFromLine validates delimiters, sender length, date length and date parse before assigning either output. An accepted sender need not be an email address. Its all-or-neither assignment prevents a valid sender from being paired with an invalid or old date. Use local temporary results and commit the related outputs after the whole parse succeeds.

Scope: Mbox envelope parser atomic state.
