# Keep correlated scan-test arrays in the same callback order

PromiseStoreScanListener appends token, sender, date and a new body slot together in onStartMessage, then appends stream chunks to that last body slot. Its separate arrays are an explicit local test-comparison choice because dates are hard to control. Preserve positional alignment in this helper; do not turn this example into a general preference for parallel arrays over objects.

Scope: Message-store scan test helper.
