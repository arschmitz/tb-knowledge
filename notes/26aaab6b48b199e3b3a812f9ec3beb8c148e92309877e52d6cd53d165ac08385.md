# Keep the animated label immediately after its input

Account Hub animation selects input:focus + label.animated and input:not(:placeholder-shown) + label.animated. The accepted auto-form template places each label after its input, keeps a matching for/ID pair, and uses an empty placeholder. Preserve this sibling relationship when inserting helper nodes; place icons and error spans after the label. Placeholder presence drives the animation state and does not replace the permanent label.

Scope: mail/themes/shared.
