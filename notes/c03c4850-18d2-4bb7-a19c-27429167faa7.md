Trigger: PlacesObservers dispatches the three registered bookmark event types.

Pending URL hashes request a later database fetch rather than immediate per-event mutation. Adds increment tracked count; removals decrement it; URL changes remove a known old GUID mapping and add tracked new URL. After any fetch or mutation arms the observer task.
