# About-logo PNG resources do not change the about-dialog SVG consumer

Entry 1278 packages about-logo.png and about-logo@2x.png for Nightly, Beta and release branding alongside the SVG. At the fixed accepted revision, the inspected Nightly about-dialog stylesheet still references about-logo.svg. Keep resource availability separate from a consumer change. In this branding family, the @2x basename marks the paired resource; this is a local naming pattern, not a rule for every image asset. Binary artwork pixels, dimensions and visual quality were not inspected, and the discussion is pending.

Scope: Thunderbird branding resources.
