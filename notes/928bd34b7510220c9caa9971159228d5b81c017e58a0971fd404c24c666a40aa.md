# Starting the next local message must not also delete the previous IMAP source message

1957 removes the per-message IMAP DeleteMessages call from WriteStartOfNewMessage. The commit reports bulk-copy overhead and moved messages returning after restart; those observations are historical author reports, not measurements or reproduced failures here. Accepted WriteStartOfNewMessage initializes parser/header state and advances the index; accepted EndMove still deletes the source array after destination success checks. Keep message-write initialization separate from move completion ownership. This does not promise atomic batch moves or recovery after every failure. This is static source and test-code inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending; source landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: IMAP to local move ownership.
