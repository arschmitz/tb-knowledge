# DoH status refresh does not await its nested label formatting

updateDoHStatus computes a mode/status label and starts the async setStatus helper without awaiting or returning its promise. That helper awaits Fluent formatting before setting the label attributes. Completion of the outer async method alone therefore does not establish that the status text is translated and applied. Resolver visibility and resolver-name attributes are separate updates. This is static completion-flow evidence; no race, accessibility announcement or UI failure was reproduced.

Scope: DoH status display completion.
