# Commands Implement/Try recovery and visible style evidence

A repair can have a committed change and matching `Tb-Try-Repair` receipt while its fixup ref still has the old expected hash. `commands/graph/try-repair.mjs` persists `pendingFixupPublication` before committing; retry reuses its receipt/source/expected-old hash, publishes atomically, and clears the receipt only on success. Never overwrite a ref moved by another worker. [Task 5]

Scope: console.
