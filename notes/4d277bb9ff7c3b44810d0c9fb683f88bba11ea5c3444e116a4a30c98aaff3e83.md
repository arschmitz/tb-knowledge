# The native-menu port flags two non-popup elements beside unflagged popups

In this historical port, native=false is placed on Calendar's menu#item-menu rather than its child item-menupopup, and on SMTP menuitem#useDefaultItem rather than its enclosing smtpPopup. A full changed-line target inventory identifies these two non-popup targets among 457 literal additions. The inspected accepted markup later removes the flags. Keep actual element identity distinct from the port title; without the paired engine consumer, this is a source target discrepancy rather than proof of ineffective native behavior or a runtime defect.

Scope: Thunderbird native menu port target scope.
