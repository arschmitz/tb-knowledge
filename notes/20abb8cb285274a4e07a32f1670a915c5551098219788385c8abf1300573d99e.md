# Retire the event-expectation wrapper together with its private state

1901 removes synthesizeMouseExpectEvent, 1902 removes its checking helper, and 1903 removes _expectEvent plus _gSeenEvent. The old design checked a single module-global observation immediately after synthesis, including a !event convention for absence. The accepted mail helper no longer exports this wrapper or holds that state. This is a concrete dependency-removal chain, not proof of a past concurrency or timing failure. Current compose callers register their own waiter before synthesis; reuse that current contract rather than copying the old assertion wrapper. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: mail event-helper cleanup.
