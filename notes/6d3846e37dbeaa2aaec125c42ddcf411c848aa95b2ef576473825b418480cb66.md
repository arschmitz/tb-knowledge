# The signoff follow-up removes a verifier and fixture fields while retaining shipping phases

The follow-up removes the registered verify_required_signoffs function, required_signoffs attributes from three signing kinds, and required_signoffs/signoff_urls fields from 11 fixtures. The accepted signing kinds still declare shipping-phase:promote, and neighboring tier/alias verifiers remain. This extends the earlier localization-transform removal. Local declarations do not prove upstream gating equivalence, successful signing or an executed release graph.

Scope: comm taskgraph verification, signing kinds and parameter fixtures.
