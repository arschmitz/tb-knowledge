# Give network fixtures valid hostnames without erasing local mailbox cases

Entry 518 removes spaces from artificial POP fixture hostnames but retains the genuine mailbox://nobody@Local%20Folders case and its case-preserving expected host. Current unit table checks spec, host, prePath and other URL parts.
