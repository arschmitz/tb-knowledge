# The detached-window timeout extension is conditional and keeps the memory-report check

The owned test-only port requests a timeout multiplier of 2 for debug, coverage, ASan or TSan configurations. It leaves ordinary optimized loading outside the condition and keeps the existing memory minimization/report collection and detached-window diagnostics. The author describes one linked failure as slow, with a qualification. The source change and historical approval do not establish that every timeout was slowness, that window references were repaired, or that this is a general timeout policy.

Scope: browser-test detached-window diagnostics.
