# Count account setup from verification outcomes and preserve its source label

Current account hub picks source/subSource, verifies config, adds the account and increments success. Real failures increment the failure label; UserCancelledException/UserSkippedError clean up and rethrow without counting a failed setup. The historical successful assertion was a commented FIXME; the newer advanced-config test now asserts a real success count. Do not mistake commented assertions for coverage or carry old gaps forward blindly.

Scope: account-creation.
