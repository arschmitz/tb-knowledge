# The shared sound helper exposes an automation-ended event, not general playback completion

NotificationSounds reuses an Audio object for the same URL, pauses and replaces it for another URL, and pauses on profile shutdown. Creating an Audio object requires a recent mail:3pane window. Its notification-audio-ended observer callback is installed only in automation and runs on natural ended events. The JSDoc's general stops wording is broader than that code; pause and shutdown do not emit the topic here. Audio.play's promise is neither returned nor handled. Keep helper completion, caller policy and audible output separate; no sound or rejection scenario ran.

Scope: Shared notification Audio lifecycle.
