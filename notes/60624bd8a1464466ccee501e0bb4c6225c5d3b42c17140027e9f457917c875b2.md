# Wire a privacy control through markup preferences tests and reported preference names

The GPC port replaces the DNT checkbox with globalPrivacyControlCheckbox bound to privacy.globalprivacycontrol.enabled. Current preference registration, browser test and MailGlue reported-pref list agree. The old DNT preference remains registered/reported even though its checkbox was removed, and the UI shows a removal help link. Removing a visible control does not prove the legacy pref or engine signal was deleted; network behavior was not inspected. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: GPC preferences integration.
