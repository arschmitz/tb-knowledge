# Thunderbird Calendar create/edit attribute-driven source loader

Gate the new route on `calendarItem.isEvent()` and `mode === "new" || mode === "modify"`; `calendar.item.editInTab` is handled earlier, preserving tab editing. Tasks therefore remain on the legacy editor. The routing browser test covers controls, double-click create/edit, templates, pref fallback, tab editing, tasks, recurrence, raw-event exclusion, and axe coverage. [Task 11]

Scope: calendar.
