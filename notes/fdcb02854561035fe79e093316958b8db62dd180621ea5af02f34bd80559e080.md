# Separate the partial-decryption warning promise from its callers

The checked inlining patch removes the generic notifyUser wrapper. displayPartialEncryptedWarning formats the four specific Fluent strings, appends notifyPartialDecrypt with critical-medium priority and one Details button, and awaits that append. Its async promise resolves with undefined after the append completes, or rejects if an awaited operation fails. The three selected direct partial-message callers still invoke the helper without awaiting it. The helper change therefore does not establish that those callers wait for the notification or handle its rejection. No tests change in this patch. A bounded search found no direct helper or notification-id reference in the selected OpenPGP browser tests; this does not exclude indirect coverage. Bug 1840861 comment1 is missing, so this is source-only knowledge with zero completed audit credit. No notification/UI test ran.

Scope: Partial inline-PGP compose warning.
