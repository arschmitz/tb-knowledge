# Avoid extra handoffs in history ingestion

The retained earlier detailed batches already included source, tests and human discussion. The later workflow added capture, reconciliation, normalization, publication and checkpoint handoffs. Ready groups waited longer in the saved comparison, although queueing and validation time were not isolated. Keep one reconciled candidate and execute publication plus checkpoint application as one guarded operation. This is a workflow repair proposal based on saved task artifacts; it does not reduce the required evidence or establish a live throughput rate.

Scope: Thunderbird history ingestion.
