# Keep retry delay, cache expiry and update history distinct

The accepted updater records successful updates, limits that history, and uses remaining cache time or a nonzero default interval after success. Failure schedules a bounded sequence of fallback intervals and increments a failure count. getRemainingCacheTime converts expiry seconds to Date.now milliseconds. Check the units at the arithmetic boundary and keep failed attempts out of successful-update history. Only this current source was inspected; no timing or rate-limit run occurred.

Scope: Notification updater retry and time units.
