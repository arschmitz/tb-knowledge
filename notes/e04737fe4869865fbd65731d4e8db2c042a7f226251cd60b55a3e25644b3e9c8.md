# Commands selected-commit diff performance

In `commands/graph/server.mjs`, cache and coalesce only immutable full 40- or 64-character commit hashes: 15-second expiry, at most 8 entries, and at most 2 MB of text plus HTML per entry. Keep abbreviated hashes and `uncommitted-changes` uncached. Preserve full `--unified=2147483647` context, rename/editor links, and context expansion. [Task 1]

Scope: console.
