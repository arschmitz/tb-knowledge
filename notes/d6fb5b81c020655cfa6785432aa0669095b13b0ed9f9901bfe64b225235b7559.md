# Commands Implement/Try recovery and visible style evidence

`try-fixup-identity.mjs` distinguishes active rebased repairs from historical stopped repairs. Match historical recovery using the `Tb-Try-Monitor` trailer, saved historical fixup hash, and stable patch ID; a unique matching ancestor may be used, but reject ambiguity, changed content, active/build-blocked repairs, dirty worktrees, and unsupported state.
