# Convert folder names at the Unicode filesystem boundary

The current mbox and Maildir creators accept UTF-8 names, hash to nsString and call nsIFile::Append with the UTF-16 safe name. The UTF-8 safe copy serves other folder identities. Recommend keeping these conversions at the actual filesystem/API boundary instead of forcing non-ASCII names through a native narrow-character API. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run.

Scope: mailnews/local/src.
