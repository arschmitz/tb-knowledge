# Do not shrink a merged message-key range when another range is contained

MsgKeySet sorts ranges by start. Its rebuild step extends cursorEnd only when the new end is greater. Assigning every overlapping range end would lose the tail of a larger containing range. The unit test exercises nested, front-extending and end-extending ranges and the final 1–150 union. This is inclusive numeric range merging; the parser assumes NNTP article numbers and is not a general interval library. This note is based on source inspection. Tests were not run, and full review discussion remains pending.

Scope: mailnews/base/src.
