# Keep recovery payloads out of default knowledge retrieval

In Commands retrieval policy v1, operational recovery, structured raw browser DOM and the two named parser-inventory studies are retained for explicit ID reads but excluded from normal search and learning. This removed about 75% of the measured search sections without removing original records or archiving lessons. Scope the exclusion by inspected record type and study tag; a large record or a source-map label alone is not a reason to exclude it. Recovery manifests can reference already saved captures after validating their IDs and receipt hashes instead of copying their payloads again. The working-tree implementation passed 62 relevant tests and 5 recovery-helper tests. Three search samples improved, but do not establish general latency. This is an observed implementation result, not an automatic change to user style rules. Original disk files remain; compacting physical storage is separate.

Scope: knowledge/retrieval.
