# Allow a considered override for a hostname mismatch after the warning

The first notification design made a hostname-mismatch alert unclickable. Bug 1903135 reported that this blocked users who had previously connected with a manually accepted certificate and that other certificate errors also permit man-in-the-middle attacks. The accepted July fix makes the warning clickable and opens the exception dialog, while retaining the mismatch warning text. Do not cite the May restriction as current policy. This correction was uplifted to beta and ESR 128.

Scope: mail certificate error flow.
