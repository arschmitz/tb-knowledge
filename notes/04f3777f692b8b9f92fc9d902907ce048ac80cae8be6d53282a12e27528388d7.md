# Separate a backed-out implementation from the underlying safety goal

851 changes allocation checks and length types; 855 is its full inverse and says rs=bustage. The current HeaderData still uses size_t and default-initialized pointer/length. A backout does not prove that unsigned lengths or allocation checks are wrong as a general rule. Keep the exact per-path inverse, current representation and unresolved causal discussion together when learning from this chain.
