# Keep the current data-collection privacy link tied to its own preference

The retired openPrivacyPolicy read toolkit.telemetry.infoURL and opened a content tab. The current Preferences path sets dataCollectionPrivacyNotice from toolkit.datacollection.infoURL through _setupLearnMoreLink, and hides the link if the formatted URL is empty. Preserve that current preference-to-element mapping instead of reviving the retired wrapper. The label retains its text-link behavior and localization ID; activation and spoken link output were not checked. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.
