# Forget interaction IDs only after their notification leaves the feed

The May 2024 cache stores IDs of notifications users dismissed or clicked and filters them from getNotifications. On feed update, it removes IDs no longer in the feed. Later bundled offline notifications required retaining their IDs too; a 2025 fix skips cleanup when loading temporary fallback data. Apply the current union and fallback rule, not the original feed-only rule.

Scope: in-app notification storage.

Paths:

- `mail/components/inappnotifications/modules/InAppNotifications.sys.mjs`
