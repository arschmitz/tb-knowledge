# Remove compose workarounds without removing the signature spacing contract

The landing deletes an old reply-on-top HTML line-break workaround reported to add an unwanted third line. Accepted ConvertAndLoadComposeWindow still checks paragraph mode and inserts one or two breaks around a signature-before-quote according to identity settings. The lesson is to trace the remaining editor mode and signature branches when retiring a workaround, not to prohibit all InsertLineBreak calls. Exact editor behavior and the reported regression were not reproduced. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Compose reply spacing.
