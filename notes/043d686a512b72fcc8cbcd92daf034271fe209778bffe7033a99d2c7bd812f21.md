# Retain the EOY content-tab change as historical behavior

Entry 1166 opens the appeal in a foreground contentTab, selects the current or most recent tabmail, focuses its owner window, and falls back to the external browser when no tabmail is available. It marks the appeal version viewed after initiating that action. Later entry 2275 removes the EOY methods and preferences in favor of in-app notifications. Do not teach the 2024 version, URL or helper as current donation behavior. This is source and test inspection only. Full Bugzilla and historical review discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran.
