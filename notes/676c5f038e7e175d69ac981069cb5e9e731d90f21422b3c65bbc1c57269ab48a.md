# Thunderbird Calendar edit-Save Bugzilla planning and date-picker research

Vanilla Calendar Pro 3.4.0 is the first-trial candidate: MIT, no runtime dependencies, supplied-container and ordinary-DOM support, modular styles/extensions, relevant visual states, and Cypress axe/keyboard tests. TheDatepicker is dependency-free but has weaker automated accessibility evidence; Zag adds a state engine/packages; wc-datepicker uses Stencil despite its marketing; USWDS is a behavior/a11y reference rather than a drop-in.
