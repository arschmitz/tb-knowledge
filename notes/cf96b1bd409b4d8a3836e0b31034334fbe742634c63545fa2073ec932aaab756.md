# The store-type Boolean migration preserves the incoming-server receiver

The initial Boolean-attribute landing calls getBoolValue directly on gAccount in the no-identities settings page. Its backout restores the prior incomingServer receiver. The reland keeps toggleAttribute but reads gAccount.incomingServer.getBoolValue and negates the two allowed-change conditions. The fixed accepted source retains that receiver and expression. This is a scoped receiver correction, not a global naming or Boolean-conversion rule. No test execution establishes the backout cause.

Scope: mailnews/base/prefs.
