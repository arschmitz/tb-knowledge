# Compose result handling waits for preparation before delivery

Compose preparation dispatches one compose-prepare-message-completed event for success or failure. Its once listener performs conversion before resolving or rejecting preparation. Result handling awaits preparation first and delivery second, instead of allowing Promise.all to reject on delivery before conversion is available. The catch resolves a still-pending delivery promise and finally removes operation observers. This ordering fits these callbacks; it is not a general ban on Promise.all or a guarantee that a missing preparation event can never hang.

Scope: Compose preparation and delivery ordering.
