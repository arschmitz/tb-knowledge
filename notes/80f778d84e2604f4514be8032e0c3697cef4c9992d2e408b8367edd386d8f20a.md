# Keep the dark-mode checkbox inverse explicit

disableDarkReader is checked when mail.dark-reader.enabled is false; the click writes the inverse of checked. A preference observer updates checked state in other message windows and schedules a reload. The clicked window also sets a Fluent title for the action. Recommend names and comments that preserve this inversion when modifying the control; a positive enabled name would require changing the binding, not just renaming it. These are fixed accepted source and test-code observations. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending.
