# The historical Yahoo/AOL notification flag records issuer discovery rather than login success

In the owned 4053 phase, mail.inappnotifications.pkceUpgradeForYahooAol defaults false. initFromHostname sets it true after provider details identify login.yahoo.com or login.aol.com and the preference is not already true. The write precedes the issuer client-ID guard and token work; no reset path is added. It therefore records matching issuer discovery, not completed authentication or a fresh account inventory. ATT is included through its Yahoo issuer mapping. The fixed accepted initialization sequence and notification defaults no longer contain this flag; keep this as historical behavior. The patch adds no login test or direct notification-delivery assertion, and the broader notification-domain proposal is outside its implementation.

Scope: mailnews/oauth/inappnotifications.
