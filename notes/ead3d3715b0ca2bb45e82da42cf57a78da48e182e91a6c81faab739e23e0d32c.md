# New-mail alert wrapper roles and delayed text visibility are separate source changes

The body carries role=alert, while the populated text container starts aria-hidden. Two XUL row wrappers are presentation elements and retain their label children. The later connected-node timer removes aria-hidden after resize handling; its 150 ms delay and these roles are source state, not proof of OS priority or a screen-reader interruption.

Scope: Legacy mail-alert accessibility markup.
