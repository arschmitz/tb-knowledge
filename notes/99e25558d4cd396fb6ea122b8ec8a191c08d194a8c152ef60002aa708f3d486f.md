# Reset each notification scenario and keep pending waits scoped to that cycle

The notification cleanup patch deletes a temporary folder that otherwise produces later alerts, resets the mock for every positive Inbox/flag case, clears a delayed click timeout and rejects pending shown/closed waits on reset. Review rejects requiring an alert before reset: showing at most one between resets matters more. The historical duplicate check becomes active when _didNotify is set, after a delayed click when configured; do not infer it catches every pre-completion overlap. The fixed shared mock has a different alert-presence check but still rejects pending waits on reset. No notification or verify run occurred.

Scope: Notification browser-test isolation.
