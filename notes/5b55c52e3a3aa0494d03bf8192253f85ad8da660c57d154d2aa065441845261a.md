# Retiring the appeal also removes its test suppression preferences

The old end-of-year appeal is removed at its preference defaults, startup timeout and helper methods. The browser-test manifest and Marionette instance stop setting its viewed-version suppression preference. The accepted main-window startup retains the separate in-app notification feature gate. This is a coordinated retirement of the old path, not evidence that its timer or first-run policy governs the replacement. Historical comm-central manual testing is reported; no beta, ESR or new-profile run was performed here.

Scope: Notification startup migration.
