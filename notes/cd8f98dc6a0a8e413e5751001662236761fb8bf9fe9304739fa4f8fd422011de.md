# The pending-header UID rename preserves the original drain and progress-total behavior

The owned folder rename changes the pending buffer to nsTArray<ImapUid> and renames its total counter across reconciliation and download consumers. The original getter still reports the saved total, appends all pending entries and removes them after delivery. It is separate from the later one-output getter that moves and resets the array and no longer exposes the total. Names and the unsigned alias do not enforce UID/key separation; current mapping placeholders remain a separate known limit. No native fetch or build was run.

Scope: IMAP pending header UID storage phases.
