# Distinguish notification interaction history from visual display

857 passes the persisted interactedWith IDs into filtering. The current getNotifications still does so, and markAsInteractedWith deduplicates and saves that list. The wire field remains displayed_notifications while the implementation names the history for interaction. Do not treat these names as evidence of an impression counter. Preserve the exact wire snake_case and the local lowerCamelCase names instead of silently renaming serialized data.

Scope: Notification wire and history naming.
