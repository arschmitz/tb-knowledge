# Commands console reliability, task recovery, storage retention, and patch identity

Tree markup is in `commands/graph/templates.mjs`. On desktop, keep the active graph panel/workspace viewport-filling, make `.graph` and `.diff-viewer` the two scroll containers, and set nested `.diff-message`, `.file-diff`, `.review-code-block`, `.review-suggestion`, and `.pretty-file` content to visible overflow with no max-height. Use the `ResizeObserver` in `commands/graph/client/init.js` to maintain `--console-header-height`; preserve the mobile one-column breakpoint below 981px. [Task 3]

Scope: console.
