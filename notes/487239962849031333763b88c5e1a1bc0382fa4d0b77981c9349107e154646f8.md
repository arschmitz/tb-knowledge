# Invalidate all calendar views before the next display after midnight

1098 iterates the actual view-box children and sets each mToggleStatus to -1 instead of enumerating four IDs and excluding the current view. That makes hidden views redraw when they are next shown. The update also refreshes the date-relative unifinder interval and today-pane button day number. The accepted tests check invalidated hidden views before opening them. Preserve the invalidation and the separate date-relative consumers.

Scope: Calendar midnight hidden-view invalidation.
