# Appearance layout controls keep preference IDs while their pane and shortcut change

The 1944986 relocation keeps mail.tabs.drawInTitlebar and mail.tabs.autoHide but moves their registration and controls to Appearance. The toolbar settings shortcut and checkbox task both target paneAppearance/layoutGroup. The checked category selector shows or hides direct paneDeck children by data-category, so moving nested markup alone is not the entire pane contract. The checkbox helper asserts stored preferences and checked states; it does not prove native window layout or search/accessibility behavior. This is scoped source knowledge from the complete relocation patch and selected fixed code; no runtime tests ran.

Scope: Preferences pane relocation contracts.
