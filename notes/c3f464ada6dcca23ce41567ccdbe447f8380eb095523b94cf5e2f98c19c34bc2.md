# Find commands now dispatch through the visible message-pane owner

The removed goUpdateFindTypeMenuItems only updated the old cmd_findTypeText and cmd_findTypeLinks commands. Accepted about3Pane registers cmd_find, cmd_findAgain and cmd_findPrevious against messagePane callbacks, enabled when its browser pane is visible. This keeps find in the current pane owner instead of the deleted menu updater. The nearby comment reports why findbars are created lazily; that later diagnostic was not reproduced or attributed to this removal. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: mail find commands.
