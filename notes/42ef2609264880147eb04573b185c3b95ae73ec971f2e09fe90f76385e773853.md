# The related-items task does not observe its async dependency assertion helper

checkLoginsMap is async but has no await, and the inspected test calls invoke it without awaiting or returning its Promise. Its assertion still executes during the call; a thrown assertion becomes a rejected returned Promise rather than a synchronous throw to the task. The callers do not observe that Promise. This is a static completion-boundary finding; the harness's unhandled-rejection behavior and a passing or failing test result were not established.

Scope: Related-items xpcshell assertion helper and its callers.
