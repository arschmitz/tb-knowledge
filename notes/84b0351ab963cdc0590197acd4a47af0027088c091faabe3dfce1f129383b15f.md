# Outgoing security-info pointer transfer requires ownership proof beyond the safety comment

Accepted SafeMsgOutgoingListener clones the security-info RefPtr and uses RefPtr::forget_into_raw before passing it to an IDL in parameter. The safety comment covers non-null/type validity but does not show who releases the forgotten reference. This is a scoped unresolved ownership check, not a confirmed leak: the paired generated binding/callee retention and Rust/C++ runtime were not established. Do not infer correct ownership merely from a SAFETY label. This is static source and test-code inspection. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.
