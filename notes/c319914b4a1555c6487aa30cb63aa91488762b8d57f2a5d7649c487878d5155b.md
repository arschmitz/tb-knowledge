# IMAP construction and shutdown share the channel-state monitor

The historical fix locked URL/channel setup and DeathSignalReceived against shutdown. The fixed accepted source now uses mMonitor, a ReentrantMonitor, to protect SetupWithUrl and channel-status inspection. It also protects m_urlInProgress through IsUrlInProgress. Favor that later contract over teaching the old mLock mutex as current. Other death/readiness fields use separate monitors; this is not a complete race audit. This is static source and test-code inspection. Complete Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: mailnews/imap/src.
