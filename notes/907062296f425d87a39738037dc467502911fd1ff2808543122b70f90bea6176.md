# Unknown signature authors are not filtered out by the signature tree

The key-details caller passes false for ignoreUnknownUid. When no acceptable signer handle is found, RNP can add userId="?" and sigKnown=false; SigListView copies the entry without reading sigKnown. This shows the display path can include an unknown signer. It does not establish whether a certification is cryptographically valid. Proposed focused test: provide a list with known and unknown signer rows and check the intended display. The inspected stripping test asserts list lengths, not unknown-signer display. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: OpenPGP signature display.
