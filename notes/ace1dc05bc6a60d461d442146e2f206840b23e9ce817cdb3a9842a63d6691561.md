# The quick-filter role patch labels its two existing roving button groups as toolbars

The three-pane document includes quickFilterBar.inc.xhtml, whose primary filter toggles and text-field scope toggles now have role=toolbar on their existing roving-group containers. The owned change does not alter button IDs, localization references or keyboard handlers. The bar and text-filter section have hidden initial states; these source attributes do not by themselves prove visible native roles, accessible names or keyboard/screen-reader behavior. No runtime test ran.

Scope: Quick-filter accessibility grouping.
