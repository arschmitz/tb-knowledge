# Keep plain-connection warnings nonblocking and validation errors separate

The manual configuration test selects None, checks the warning is visible and confirms Continue remains enabled. Hostname/port/username validation still owns its own invalid and described-by errors, clearing them on valid input. Preserve the difference between a security warning and a blocking form error; their similar alert styling does not make them the same permission. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending.
