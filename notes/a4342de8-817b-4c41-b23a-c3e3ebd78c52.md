# Do not offer a certificate override for a domain mismatch

A certificate domain mismatch may indicate interception. In the May 2024 certificate alert flow, the domain mismatch notification is not clickable, so it cannot open the exception dialog. Other overridable certificate errors show a notification that can open the dialog when the user clicks it.

Scope: mail certificate alerts.

Paths:

- `mail/components/activity/modules/alertHook.sys.mjs`
