# Thunderbird clang-format CI delegates through the commlint linter wrapper

The source-test job invokes commlint -l clang-format and fetches clang-tidy. Its configuration uses commlint:lint_wrapper with wraps: clang-format:lint. The local wrapper applies exclusions/support paths before delegating. This replaces the dedicated shell task and its diff-size exit check; it does not establish unchanged upstream behavior or a successful lint run.

Scope: Taskcluster clang-format lint integration.
