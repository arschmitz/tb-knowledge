# Local message injection can require a FolderLoaded completion wait

The current injection helper updates every affected local folder after message additions unless doNotForceUpdate is set. updateFolderAndNotify registers its listener before updateFolder, matches FolderLoaded by URI, removes the listener and calls the completion function. The helper also has distinct IMAP update/download behavior. Scoped test recommendation: await the relevant folder completion after injection rather than assuming the local copy has made all dependent views/indexers ready. The IMAP-only wording at the beginning of the method documentation is stale for this local path. This is static source and test-code inspection at the frozen accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator timeline and inline discussion remain pending; a landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: message injection local folder completion.
