# The picker delay is an intermittent workaround with a remaining verification gap

Entry 1196 introduces a 250 ms delay before opening the date/time picker after a document loads. The accepted helper retains that delay and arms the picker promise before either showPicker or the internal picker-button click. The commit message reports a separate mailbox showPicker failure under --verify as still unresolved. Treat the delay as a documented workaround for this picker path, not a general timing rule or proof that the intermittent is fixed. No picker or --verify run was performed; review discussion is pending.

Scope: Form-picker browser tests.
