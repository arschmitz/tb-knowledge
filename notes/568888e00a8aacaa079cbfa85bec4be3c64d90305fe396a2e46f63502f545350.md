# Arm the feed scroll completion event before sending the key

The landing-era feed scroll test synthesized Space before installing the content scroll listener. The accepted dedicated browser_feedBodyScroll.js now installs scrollend before the key, waits for completion, checks forward progress and bottom extent, then prearms browserLoaded before the final key that advances to another article. Recommend this source-backed ordering for this test family. The earlier backout messages do not establish that the listener order caused the failures. These are fixed accepted source and test-code observations. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending.
