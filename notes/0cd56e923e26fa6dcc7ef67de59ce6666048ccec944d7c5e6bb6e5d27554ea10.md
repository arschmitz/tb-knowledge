# The v2.0.1 background handlers stop opening options while the flow comment remains

The v1.9.2 background opens the options page after completed sign-in, bridge delivery and OPEN_MANAGEMENT_PAGE. The v2.0.1 handlers remove those calls; the completed-FTUE sign-in path still closes the login tab and initializes cloud files. Its flow comment continues to say options opens. The fixed v2.0.16 receiver and comment retain this bounded mismatch. This is static handler/comment evidence, not a reproduced login defect or proof that no other caller can open management.

Scope: Thundermail background login and management routing.
