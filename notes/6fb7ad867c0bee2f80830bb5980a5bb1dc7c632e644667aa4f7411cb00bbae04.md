# Keep the explicit POP3 deletion state outside the retention-policy conjunction

A scoped manual control-expression recommendation for _actionUidlResponse is to preserve the parentheses around KEEP plus retention policy, then OR that result with explicit DELETE. Otherwise a user-requested deletion could depend on leaveMessagesOnServer or age. This is supported by the accepted local expression; it is not a global preference for a particular operator style.
