# CalDAV yields preserve the surrounding handler’s processing boundary

The accepted multiget counter yields after ten changed-item additions, while the separately inspected WebDav sync loop yields after twenty-five handled responses. Both keep their counters on the handler and reset after yielding. For a new responsiveness change, choose the relevant completed unit of work in that handler; copying a nearby numerical threshold does not prove equivalent behavior or performance.
