# HTML META charset storage is released during finalization after parsed-HTML EOF work

The parsed-HTML subclass calls the HTML parent's EOF before it later invokes that parent's parse_line on serialized buffered HTML. parse_line can then retain a PL_strndup META charset allocation. The patch removes the earlier EOF free and registers a finalizer that frees the stored charset, nulls the field and delegates upward. Preserve that ownership and ordering rather than equating EOF with the last use. This explains the inspected path only; historical ASan reports are attributed and no all-leaks or fresh sanitizer result is established.

Scope: MIME HTML allocation lifetime.
