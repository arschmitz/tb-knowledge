# The card-color cleanup preserves separate shared, junk and forced-colors state paths

The landing removes ordinary card-color duplicates from threadCard.css and changes shared current, selected and hover/focus variables in tree-listbox.css. Mail junk overrides and its unfocused selected/current hover branch remain in threadCard.css. Forced-colors has its own palette. The ordinary dark tree-view token becomes black, but the fixed accepted thread-row receiver later uses layout-background-0; the original change does not make every message-list presentation black. The existing shared-tree versus mail-state lesson remains the architectural context. No rendered contrast, native accessibility or selection behavior was tested.

Scope: Thunderbird tree and card selection color phases.
