# Bind account-editing state to its utility instance

AccountManagerUtils takes its account in the constructor and holds it in private #account. This avoids a mutable shared utility whose methods silently use whichever account was assigned last. D211687 requested either constructor-bound state or explicit account parameters and adopted the class. Current account and RSS settings share that utility.
