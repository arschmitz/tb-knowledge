# An unavailable initial calendar can coexist with selectable choices

For D332509 at revision 3d29fe1d078b26b2f91ee102bc82cfd2308867f1, the AI assessment reports that the fallback branch displays the initial calendar when it is outside viewModel.calendars, while other allowed calendars can remain selectable. This matters when editing an event from a hidden calendar: an unavailable initial choice does not mean all calendar choices are unavailable. The reviewer questioned the word “destination”; replacing it with “allowed calendar choice” was proposed but not accepted or applied. The selector browser test pass is AI-reported.

Scope: Calendar event calendar selector.
