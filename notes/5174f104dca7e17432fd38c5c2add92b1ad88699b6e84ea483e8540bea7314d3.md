# Keep provider identity stable through re-registration

ExtSearchBook defaults its name to extension.name and its ID to a generated UID when arguments are absent. Registration rejects a duplicate UID, and a persistent listener re-registered with changed parameters after termination is explicitly rejected. Preserve identity and argument consistency across restart. The new tests exercise a duplicate listener and removal of a dummy book; they do not fully exercise the changed-parameter error path. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Extension provider identity contract.
