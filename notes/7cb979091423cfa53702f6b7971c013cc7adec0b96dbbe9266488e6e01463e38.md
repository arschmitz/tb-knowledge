# AuthEnveloped MIME admission updates both the classifier and decoder sanity check

This landing adds authEnveloped-data in two local admission points: the classifier treats it like enveloped-data in the existing signed-parent branch, and the CMS initializer no longer rejects that smime-type string. The surrounding layer policy and later decoded-content guards remain separate. Decoder creation/start and accepted EOF state checks do not by themselves establish successful decryption or authenticated content. The underlying CMS implementation and linked D288495 were not paired, and no crypto/MIME test ran.

Scope: S/MIME dispatch.
