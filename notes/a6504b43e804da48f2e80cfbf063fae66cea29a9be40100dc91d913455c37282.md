# Saved event IDs and editable calendar ID have separate roles

For D332509 at revision 3d29fe1d078b26b2f91ee102bc82cfd2308867f1, the AI inspection reports that snapshotEvent preserves source calendar, event, and occurrence IDs, while cloneDraftFromSourceSnapshot creates editable values. Changing the draft calendar does not change the IDs that identify the saved event. This distinction can guide comments and changes at the source-snapshot boundary. The reviewer requested simpler wording; the proposed rewrite was not accepted or applied. Passing tests are reported by the AI, without independent execution evidence.
