# The Exchange browser account-creation case exercises password authentication

The new browser full-account-creation case selects passwordCleartext against its EWS fixture and checks the resulting account and identity. Custom OAuth is covered separately by component capture assertions and xpcshell native-property assertions on an EWS account. Those direct checks do not establish a successful browser OAuth/token flow or Graph account creation. Keep exercised paths and asserted outcomes separate; this study executed no tests.

Scope: Account Hub Exchange submission test boundaries.
