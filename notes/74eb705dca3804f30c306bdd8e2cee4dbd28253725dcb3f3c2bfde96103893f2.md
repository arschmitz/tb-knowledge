# Keep WebExtension link policy values separate from native actor groups

The public tabs, windows and spaces schemas accept strict, balanced and relaxed. getMessageManagerGroup maps them to single-page, single-site and browsers, with balanced/default choosing single-site. Creation callers apply the native value to tabs or popup browsers. Recommend keeping the public camelCase linkHandler property and shared conversion helper rather than duplicating internal group strings across APIs. These are fixed accepted source and test-code observations. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint, live accessibility or crypto/TLS validation ran.

Scope: mail/components/extensions.
