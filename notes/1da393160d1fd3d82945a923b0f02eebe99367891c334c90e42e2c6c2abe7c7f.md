# The old search-extension removal also deletes its resource registration and duplicate allowances

This landing deletes the legacy search-extension manifests, locale data and icons together with jar.mn, its JAR_MANIFESTS entry and the related installer duplicate-file allowance groups. At the fixed accepted revision, the search component still packages its OS integration modules, while the separate settings build lists search-config-v2.json and search-config-icons.json. These are comm resource/build facts. The unpaired upstream engine loader, default-engine choice, profile migration and provider/network equivalence were not verified.

Scope: Search resource packaging.
