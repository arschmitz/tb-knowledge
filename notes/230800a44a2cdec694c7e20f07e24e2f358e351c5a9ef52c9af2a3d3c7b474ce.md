# Space tests add content-body coverage without proving exact scroll timing or destination identity

The accepted browser_spacehit tests cover preference on/off and Shift/non-Shift in both outer about:message and focused content body. The body helper sends three presses with 250 ms waits and compares old/new message identity, not exact scroll positions or a specific expected previous/next header. Retain those assertion limits when citing the test; its body-focus assertion is structural source evidence, not live keyboard or accessibility validation. This is static source and test-code inspection. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.
