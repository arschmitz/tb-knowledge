# Preserve the Account Hub naming contracts across host and shadow controls

The accepted forms use lowerCamel IDs such as incomingConnectionSecurity and the host creates an internal Select-suffixed ID for its native select/label. Dashed account-hub-select, l10n-label-id/l10n-error-id attributes and account-hub-select-security-warning Fluent keys serve different layers. Recommend preserving this local mapping and checking label for/aria targets instead of converting every identifier to one format. These observations are scoped to the cited accepted source and historical changes. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline discussion remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run.
