# Calendar provider tests require item batches but no longer assert onLoad is outside a batch

The updated provider helpers always require onAddItem and onModifyItem at batch count one. The unit helper removes its onLoad count-zero assertion and retains three expected-calendar identity assertions without messages. These are distinct coverage and diagnostic limits; the test changes do not establish every callback, failure or cancellation order. The deliberate message rule applies to those missing messages, not to a harmless absence of should alone.

Scope: Calendar provider observer test coverage.
