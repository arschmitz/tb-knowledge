# Treat folder row ARIA text as derived asynchronous state

A folder row's accessible label depends on its name, unread count, total count, and size. Schedule an update when these inputs change and guard delayed updates against newer state, or an older update can overwrite the label after a later change.

Scope: folder-pane.
