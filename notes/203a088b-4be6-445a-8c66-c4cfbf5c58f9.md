# Choose new-message tab focus from the action before the user preference

For message opening, middle click and the context-menu Open in New Tab action start in the background regardless of mail.tabs.loadInBackground. Shift reverses that choice and focuses the tab. Double-click or normal open still follows the preference, with Shift reversing it. MailUtils.displayMessages routes forceTab to openMessageInNewTab; backgroundCmd overrides the preference and shiftPressed determines inversion. The separate OpenMessageInNewTab helper accepts an explicit background flag from mailContext, then applies Shift even when a flag was supplied. The accepted browser tests remove pref toggles for middle click and check the selected tab. Current source retains these paths.

Scope: Message tab behavior.
