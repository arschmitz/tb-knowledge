# Simple MIME finalization adds superclass cleanup with an EOF re-entry assumption

Finalize deletes and nulls its buffer before invoking the inline-text superclass. That chain reaches leaf/object cleanup, including header release, but also calls the concrete EOF handler. EndGather returns early when closed; an unclosed invocation can reach the cleared fields after its own superclass EOF succeeds. The checked free and failed-initializer entries do not independently establish prior closure. Preserve that lifecycle gap alongside the new cleanup route; neither the source nor historical review confidence proves every error path safe. No sanitizer or failure-path test ran.

Scope: Simple MIME converter superclass cleanup.
