The original renamed helper obtains GetMessageKey and casts it to ImapUid in its partial-fetch/show-deleted/just-expunged branch. Its TODO explicitly defers a real UID lookup. Keep that real mapping phase separate from the naming/type-alias change. No expunge, mapping or server test ran.
