# Manual S/MIME fixtures preserve intentional bad outer signatures during refresh

The manual fixture instructions intentionally retain a mismatched outer signature while replacing the inner encrypted block. This landing adds copying the generated source Date too. The two bad-signature fixture payloads change while their outer signature bytes stay unchanged. Accepted later automation follows that boundary by replacing only the first enveloped-data block and Date and leaving the trailing signature alone. A static byte match does not establish decryption or signature validity, and the exact backout remains a separate phase.

Scope: S/MIME layered manual fixtures.
