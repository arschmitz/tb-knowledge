# A shutdown barrier rename also needs its callback discriminator

The first port renamed the AsyncShutdown barrier but left BlockShutdown testing quit-application-granted. The corrected reland tests quit-application. In the initial source an unmatched phase falls into Shutdown rather than CleanupOnExit; the author’s did-nothing wording describes the missed cleanup, not a literal empty callback. The separate quit-application-granted observer topic stays valid. At the fixed accepted endpoint this early barrier is gone: the observer runs CleanupOnExit and the remaining profile-before-change blocker runs Shutdown. Check registration and callback dispatch together when porting a phase. No shutdown run or paired Gecko test was performed.

Scope: Account shutdown phases.
