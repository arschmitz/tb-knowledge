# The Copies and Folders picker test separates URI selection from folder creation and protocol behavior

The new test runs sent, archive, draft and template choices through empty, standard-name, alternate-name and nested local POP accounts. It directly checks identity destination URIs and flag movement, including that the empty target folder still does not exist. The popup helper waits for shown and hidden events. These assertions exercise selection and local flags; they do not cover Exchange flag locking, a real sent-copy operation, restart persistence or live accessibility.

Scope: Copies and Folders browser test coverage.
