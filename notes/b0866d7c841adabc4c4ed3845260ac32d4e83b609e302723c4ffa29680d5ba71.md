# Prefer the newer passwordless predicate over the historical OAuth-only gate

The historical isOauthOnly implementation required OAuth2 on both incoming and outgoing servers. Current isOauthOnly also accepts outgoing settings derived from incoming, and the email view now uses usesPasswordlessAuthentication for none, GSSAPI and OAuth2 combinations. Tests cover mixed OAuth/GSSAPI and derived outgoing cases. Do not reuse the original two-server OAuth condition as the whole current passwordless rule. This is source and test-code inspection. Complete Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: mail/components/accountcreation.
