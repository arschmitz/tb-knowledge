# Remove the dormant interested-listener wrapping path while preserving logger setup

The historical false flag gated optional xpcshell wrapping and the initial structured lifecycle/start info message. The patch removes that dormant conditional. Logger instances and the console tunnel were already initialized outside that conditional. Current _init_log_helper still creates both console instances and initializes the tunnel. This does not prove every console level is printed or a remote logger is connected. Do not preserve the deleted flag as a required logger setup condition. This is static source and test-code inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending; source landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: mail test logging.
