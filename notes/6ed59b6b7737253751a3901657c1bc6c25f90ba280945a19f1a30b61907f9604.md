# Rebuild the application-action menu after selection and cancellation

chooseApp stops the selection event from reaching the normal handler. Its shared completion callback rebuilds the menu on both success and cancellation; only a selected handler causes matching, reselection and onSelectAction. Keep this outcome distinction when editing the application picker. A focused proposed test should confirm that cancellation restores the previous action rather than storing the picker placeholder. No cancellation failure was reproduced. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: mail handler picker UI.
