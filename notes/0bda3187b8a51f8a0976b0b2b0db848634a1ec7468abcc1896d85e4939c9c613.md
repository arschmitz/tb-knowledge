# Subscription search visibility belongs to the complete labeled control

In the 2024 subscription dialog, entry 1190 hides nameContainer when the server does not support subscribe search. This hides the label and input together instead of leaving an orphan label. Treat that as the historical capability check, not a current IMAP restriction: the fixed accepted dialog now calls its own SubscribeDataAdapter.filter and retains the search control. For capability-limited forms, hiding the complete labeled group is a scoped recommendation. Keyboard focus and accessibility behavior were not run; the historical unconditional gNameField.focus also means the focus outcome was not proved. Full bug and review discussion remains pending.

Scope: Subscription dialog controls.
