# Apply the week-start preference before refreshing the calendar view

CalendarBaseView handles calendar.week.start by storing the new preference and then calling refreshView. Its observer forwards calendar preferences to the view handler. Current initialization reads weekStartOffset inside ensureInitialized rather than the 2024 connectedCallback. Preserve the order of preference read before refresh. The later initialization placement is current evidence only; this batch does not cover that later landing or prove its failure cause.

Scope: calendar/base/content.
