# Calendar Past and All ranges have finite recurrence bounds

740 adds Past Events and All Events to the unifinder. Past begins 100 years before today; All spans 100 years before and after today. Today is made a date, and these date-range choices follow the end-date boundary used by the filter. These finite ranges avoid asking recurring calendars for an unbounded occurrence set. The UI label All is not a promise to enumerate every event across unlimited time. No provider performance benchmark was run.

Scope: Calendar unifinder ranges.
