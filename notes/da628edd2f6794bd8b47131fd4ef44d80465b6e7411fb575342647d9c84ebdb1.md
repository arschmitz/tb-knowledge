# Document SearchSpec ownership separately from search-term types

SearchSpec takes ownership of viewTerms and userTerms and can mutate their grouping flags. It clones virtualFolderTerms because they come from persistent definitions. The getters expose current terms with a do-not-mutate contract. Preserve those ownership statements when changing these setters or their documentation; accurate array types alone do not describe the side effects.

Scope: mail/modules/SearchSpec.sys.mjs.
