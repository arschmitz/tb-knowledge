# NNTP delivery waits for its callback before the send promise settles

The sender awaits _deliverAsNews, which waits for a dedicated listener promise. OnStopRunningUrl awaits delivery processing before resolving or rejecting by status. A callback rejection would bypass those later settlement statements; it was not exercised here. Native send failure closes progress without doing the success-only FCC lookup. The changed mock reports immediate success only, and its existing assertions inspect file/account selection rather than the failure dialog. Later QA success is historical evidence, not a new run.

Scope: News delivery callback and compose progress.
