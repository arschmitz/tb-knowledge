# Exchange candidate selection does not guarantee a zero-expunged folder will compact

Current CompactAll skips virtual descendants, reads databaseOpen and admits a folder when its DB is closed or expunged bytes are positive. Its comment warns a closed DB count can be stale. Shared AsyncCompactFolders then skips zero expunged bytes and unsupported stores. Thus closed-DB admission is a candidate-selection policy, not proof a reparse or compaction occurs. Proposed focused check: closed-DB/zero-count and virtual-folder cases across the caller and shared filter, without inventing a reproduced defect. This is static source and test-code inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending; source landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: Exchange compaction filtering.
