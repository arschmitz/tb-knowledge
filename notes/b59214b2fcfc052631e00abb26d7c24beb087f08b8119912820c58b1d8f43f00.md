# Keep the beta version-bump file list separate from release and ESR

The 2024 transform resolved keyed fields and disabled its ESR bump path. That transform no longer exists at the fixed accepted revision. Current release-version-bump uses the Lando transform and bumps version_display.txt by default; release and esr.* bump both version.txt and version_display.txt. Preserve the current release-type mapping. Do not carry the historical ESR-disabled rule forward.
