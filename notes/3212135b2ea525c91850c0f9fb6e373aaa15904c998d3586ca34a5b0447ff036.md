# Refresh midnight markers without moving a deliberately selected date

doMidnightUpdate moves TodayPane and the sidebar selection only when their showsToday intent is true. Otherwise it calls showMonth to redraw the current-day mark and keeps the chosen date/month; it calls goToDay only on an initialized current view. Current source also updates the miniday dropdown when TodayPane is browsing another date, a later refinement beyond the initial landing. Keep selected date and the day marked as today as distinct state.

Scope: Calendar midnight selection versus today markers.
