# Offline stream wrappers preserve the channel request identity for their consumer

The accepted native wrapper forwards mChannel as the request argument for start, data and stop callbacks. Its comment explicitly says that the need for this choice was uncertain and copied from an existing cache listener. Preserve the observed identity contract when changing this wrapper, but do not turn that comment into a general XPCOM rule without checking consumers. This is a scoped source observation with unresolved rationale; no docshell, indexing or calendar stream consumer was executed. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: IMAP offline stream request identity.
