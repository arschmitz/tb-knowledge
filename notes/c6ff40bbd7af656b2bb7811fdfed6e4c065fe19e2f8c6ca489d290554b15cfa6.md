# State lock ownership in the internal stream helper comment

The small human follow-up clarifies that Emit assumes a thread-safe caller and that the caller acquires mLock when needed. Accepted Write and Close lock before delegating; InternalClose explicitly requires the lock already held. A scoped comment rule is to name the lock and its owner at an internal boundary. Do not describe Emit itself as acquiring the lock. This is static source evidence. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Mbox stream lock ownership.
