# Keep Calendar link routing tied to the MailLink actor context

The historical Calendar patch avoids double handling by skipping mid, mailto, news and snews links for the MailLink actor, and removes its old URL allowlist. Review reports mailto/mid success while the author says file-link sanitization still needs separate work. The fixed dispatcher narrows the skip to content browsing contexts and restores direct mid handling elsewhere. Preserve that ownership boundary; the old unconditional skip is not a rule for every Calendar caller, and no arbitrary-protocol success or safety was tested.

Scope: Calendar external-link dispatch.
