# Native form accent follows the app token only outside contrast and the OS-accent choice

The original and accepted variables.css set accent-color from selected-item-color only inside not (prefers-contrast) and when mail.appearance.accentColor is not accent-color. The declaration targets both :host and :root. Other branches choose the selected foreground/background pair, including system pairs under contrast. This is the scoped native-control integration, not proof that every widget or palette meets contrast or that the manual hot-pink plan ran.

Scope: appearance-css.
