# Schedule a refresh at the next notification start time

Filtering out future notifications is insufficient by itself: an offline app may never refresh when start_at arrives. The October 2024 fix finds the next future start_at and sets a timer to recompute candidates. The review requested a @type comment and spacing after early returns; those were small readability requests in this function, not project-wide formatter rules.
