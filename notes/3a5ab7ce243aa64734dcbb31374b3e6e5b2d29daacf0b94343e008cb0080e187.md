# Tree row subclasses must preserve fill cancellation and accessibility work even when optimizing

ThreadRow and ThreadCard call super.fillRow at the accepted endpoint, while AutoTreeViewTableRow intentionally does not, because one TreeDataAdapter.rowAt call supplies the needed data. Its replacement explicitly cancels the frame and fills position/role-related attributes itself. Preserve the base work or its documented equivalent when optimizing a subclass; the 2024 call-super pattern is not an unconditional rule for every later subclass. This is static source and test-code inspection. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: mail/base/content.
