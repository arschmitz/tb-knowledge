# Refresh crafted encrypted fixtures without removing the defect they model

The smime-manual instructions replace the Date header and inner enveloped-data payload while leaving the trailing outer signature block untouched. Its mismatch is deliberate. The outer-smime-bad-sig-inner-smime-enc[-sig].eml names describe layer order and signature state; the alternate alice.* names use their older dotted fixture family. Preserve the fixture meaning and nearby naming family when refreshing keys or dates. Do not repair an intentionally invalid outer signature or make a negative decryption test fail for an unrelated expired key. Encrypted payload bytes were captured, not independently decrypted in this study.

Scope: Cryptographic fixture maintenance and file naming.
