# Manual protocol switching changes field constraints before capturing the selected config

The shared incoming form selects its protocol before capturing protocol-specific fields. Switching also hides irrelevant groups, remembers required inputs and clears stale custom-validity and ariaInvalid state. The original EWS widget accepts password (3) or OAuth2 (10) and selects 3 otherwise. The accepted later widget also accepts value 6 and Graph; those are separate phases. Edited-input completeness and native form validity are separate from connection verification. The controller unwraps incoming state.config and routes Exchange config past outgoing UI. Source tests check form transitions and password-step routing, not a successful EWS login.

Scope: account-hub-manual-protocol-switching.
