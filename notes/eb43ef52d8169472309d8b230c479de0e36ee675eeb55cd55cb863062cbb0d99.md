# Chat account deletion separates account teardown from login-store completion

This landing captures the password URI and normalized name before unInit deletes protocol state. It then removes the account/plugin/contact/preference state without spinning an event loop and starts _removePasswordInternal separately. The inspected service and mail bridge call this synchronous deletion path; they do not receive its login-removal promise. Account removal and the later login-store operation therefore have distinct local completion boundaries. The new browser task checks final account/login values, but its microtask cleanup and timed dialog steps do not prove a general scheduling guarantee. No test or login-store failure was run.

Scope: chat account lifecycle.
