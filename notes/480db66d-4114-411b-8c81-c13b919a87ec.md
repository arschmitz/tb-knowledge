# Keep shown IDs while bundled notifications fill a temporary cache gap

When the app loads bundled offline notifications as temporary fallback data, it must not prune interactedWith IDs or targeting seeds for feed items absent from that fallback. The May 2025 fix adds skipCleanup for that path; ordinary feed updates still clean IDs against feed and bundled defaults. This prevents a previously seen notification from appearing again after recovery.

Scope: in-app notification storage.

- `mail/components/inappnotifications/modules/InAppNotifications.sys.mjs`
