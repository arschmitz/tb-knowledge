# Follow focus through both notification shadow roots

InAppNotification.focus forwards to its container, whose focus method targets the focusable inner div. The manager’s Alt+Shift+J path calls the outer notification focus method. Current tests explain why document.activeElement is the outer host and check that opening/closing a notification preserves the previous search-bar focus. Preserve the inner focus target and the host-level assertion distinction. The synthesized shortcut test is not a complete keyboard-only traversal, and the CTA/close callback tests skip in automation.

Scope: mail/components/inappnotifications.
