# Keep retired account-wizard hooks separate from the surviving NNTP wizard

1938 removes a POP-wizard post-creation hook that selected Inbox, focused the window and queued MsgGetMessage, then cleared gNewAccountToLoad. 1941 removes an unused debug hook that toggled the XUL cache preference. The accepted accountUtils opens the NNTP wizard through AccountWizard.xhtml and opens email setup through Account Hub. AccountWizard still installs wizard-page handlers. These removals do not establish that account creation, email fetching or wizard use disappeared. The gNewAccountToLoad declaration remains in the inspected file; no dynamic/global usage or default mail-fetch policy is inferred. This is static source and test-code inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending; source landing does not prove approval of a proposed manual rule.

Scope: account setup legacy boundaries.
