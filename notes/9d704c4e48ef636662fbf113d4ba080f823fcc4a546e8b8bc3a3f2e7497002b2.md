# Do not claim every IMAP shutdown-field access is protected

The accepted main loop retains direct m_threadShouldDie reads after leaving monitor blocks, and the header declares an ordinary bool. DeathSignalReceived also returns false early on a failed mock-channel status before the final death-flag check. These exact branches bound the conclusions from the two historical race fixes. A full synchronization or cancellation audit needs additional callers and execution; no new race is confirmed here. This is static source and test-code inspection. Complete Bugzilla and historical review timeline/inline context remain pending. No Thunderbird runtime, build, lint or live accessibility validation ran.

Scope: mailnews/imap/src.
