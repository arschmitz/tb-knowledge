# Include owned English resources in the shared localization artifact

Entry 1063 adds COMM_ENUS_PATTERNS for Calendar, Chat, both branding families and Mail. merge_repos rewrites owned locales/en-US paths into l10n-central/en-US while also adding the translated Gecko and comm paths. The desktop translator adds en-US to the requested locales. The commit subject says tar.xz; current producer and fetch declarations use strings_all.tar.zst. Check actual producer/consumer names rather than carrying the subject spelling into a rule. This change does not claim every Gecko English resource is included.
