# The minimonth grid role belongs to its inner calendar table

The minimonth fragment assigns role=grid to its inner HTML table while retaining existing header cells and focus handling. setFocusedDate separately updates the roving tabindex and moves focus only under its own condition. This one-line markup change adds no keyboard handler or direct role assertion. Inspect semantics and focus behavior as separate contracts; no accessible-tree or screen-reader result was measured.

Scope: Calendar minimonth semantic markup.
