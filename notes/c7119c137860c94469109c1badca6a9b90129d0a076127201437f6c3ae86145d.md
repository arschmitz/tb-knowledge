# Removing the identity folder-picker getter preserves account context in the editor

1936 removes getAccountForFolderPickerState, whose only job was returning gAccount. Current identity editing still obtains account and identity from window.arguments, initializes copies/addressing/encryption from them and falls back to account.defaultIdentity when there is no identity. Do not interpret removal of this unused getter as removal of the editor’s account dependency. No broader naming or design rule is justified by this one deletion. This is static source inspection. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check was run. Full Bugzilla and Phabricator discussion remains pending.

Scope: Identity editor account context.
