# Compose-window readiness includes editor readiness after window activation

_wait_for_compose_window can wait for activate or a five-second forced-focus timeout, clears that timeout, then waits for composeEditorReady and one turn of the event loop. A loaded-window promise alone is not the complete current helper contract. Scoped test guidance: retain editor readiness when replacing or simplifying this helper. The forced-focus fallback is not evidence that normal focus behavior passed, and no timing or focus test was executed. This is static source and test-code inspection at the fixed accepted revision. No runtime, build, lint or live accessibility check ran. Full Bugzilla and historical review timeline and inline discussion remain pending.

Scope: compose helper readiness.
