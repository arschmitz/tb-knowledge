# Await favicon storage before completing the icon update path

Entries 987 and 993 adapt to promise-returning favicon storage. Current MailUtils awaits storage in both its data-URI and network paths and catches failures at its helper boundary. specialTabs.setFavIcon awaits that helper before updating the tab icon. FeedUtils awaits storage before requesting the saved favicon and returns an empty result on a corrupt-image storage rejection. A helper that logs and consumes a rejection has a different contract from one that propagates it; do not assume every awaited call rejects to its caller.

Scope: mail/modules/MailUtils.sys.mjs.
