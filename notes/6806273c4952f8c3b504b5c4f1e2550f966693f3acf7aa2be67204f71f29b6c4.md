# The new normal and unobtrusive hp assertions omit their failure messages

The normal-send helper calls Assert.ok for absence of hp with no message. The unobtrusive helper calls Assert.equal for hp=clear with no message. Both are new assertions in this patch and remain in accepted source. Each needs a nonempty message naming the expected parameter state, with should when written or repaired. Other format/body assertions and nearby comments do not supply those missing arguments. No failing test was executed.

Scope: Compose format-test assertion messages.
