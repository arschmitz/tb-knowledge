# The POP3 move backout exposed a classification-notification gap as well as DB attachment ordering

The source-only backout names test_quarantineFilterMove.js; the full tracker later attributes its wait to a msgsClassified notification not sent by the new copy-service route. The author separately explains that incoming headers were not yet attached to the database table, so copying and deleting the original had different behavior. These are attributed diagnoses, not reproduced failures. The inspected test waits for both classification and POP3 completion, then streams message contents before and after another copy. Keep those checks distinct. The proposed DB-before-filter WIP is later context, not the assigned backout or a proven complete fix. No new runtime validation.

Scope: POP3 filter moves.
