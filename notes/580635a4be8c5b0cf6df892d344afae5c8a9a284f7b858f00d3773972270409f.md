# Shared lesson citations are checked before export

At TB Tools revision f2bd7b158ee4d3386e20389d99d7c19fee54eda6, push-enabled knowledge sync rejects a shared lesson if its cited evidence is unavailable, private, or from another repository. It also rejects quotes whose record IDs are absent from the evidence list or whose exact text is absent from the cited record. These checks preserve citation scope and exact excerpts before export. They do not verify that the evidence is true or that the lesson's interpretation is correct; this validation block does not run during pull-only sync.
